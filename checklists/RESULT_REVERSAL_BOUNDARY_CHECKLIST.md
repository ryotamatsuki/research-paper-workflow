# Result Reversal, Paradox, Impossibility and Sharp-Boundary Checklist

> Unreleased post-v2.9 MINOR-class companion to existing Stages 2/3/4/4A/6/7.5A/11. This is not a new Stage, automatic novelty certificate, or change to GO/NO-GO routing.

## A. Predecessor theorem and comparability

- [ ] Identify primary source/version, main theorem and proof location, actual sign/ranking/monotonicity/implementation/existence conclusion, exact quantifiers and domain.
- [ ] Record players, information, strategy sets, timing, equilibrium selection, participation, constraints, and welfare/planner definition used in the predecessor.
- [ ] Check whether a proposed reversal compares the **same** outcome, information, feasible policies, social welfare/accounting, equilibrium concept and selection. A changed object is not automatically a contradiction.
- [ ] Inspect author-stated limitations and later same-author, citing, working-paper and generic-parent results. Label provenance `AUTHOR-STATED / AUDITOR-INFERRED / CROSS-PAPER`; specify source location, reading depth and search cutoff.

## B. Bounded discovery (Stages 2/3)

- [ ] Explore, alongside new questions and generalizations, (i) comparative-static sign reversal, (ii) welfare or institutional ranking reversal, (iii) non-monotonicity, (iv) counterintuitive allocation, (v) impossibility and minimal counterexamples, (vi) sharp necessary/sufficient validity boundary, and (vii) unexpectedly strong robustness/invariance.
- [ ] **Permit the original economic research question to remain unchanged.** New theorems in known models, substantial generalizations and corrected scope can be publishable without invented mechanisms.
- [ ] Propose at most one economically motivated essential assumption/margin change per first test; state the exact restriction that reproduces the predecessor; a new theorem in an unchanged model is also permissible.
- [ ] Pre-specify a falsifiable mathematical claim, a rival `NO REVERSAL / ABSORBED / STRONGER ROBUSTNESS` possibility, expected specialist learning, and minimal proof/counterexample test.
- [ ] Check generic parent theorem, post-publication boundary results and closest alternate terminology; a missing search hit is not proof of an open gap.
- [ ] Set a finite search/computation budget; no repeated ad hoc changes to force the desired sign. Keep `HYPOTHESIS / LITERATURE-SCREENED / MODEL-TESTED / CERTIFIED / KILLED` separate from `RESOLVED / PARTIALLY RESOLVED / OPEN WITH EVIDENCE / UNRESOLVED`.

## C. Construction, certification and scope (Stages 4/4A/7.5A)

- [ ] **Reproduce the predecessor first** on its true assumptions, domain and equilibrium-selection convention.
- [ ] Change only the Stage-3-authorized assumption and **re-solve the full modified game** including applicable corners, regimes, deviations, existence, multiplicity and off-path continuations.
- [ ] Derive an exact counterexample or theorem and seek a sharp boundary or open parameter region if justified. Label isolated/knife-edge examples honestly; a material counterexample may still be a result within correct scope.
- [ ] Independently reconstruct a high-stakes payoff/welfare comparison; use existing formal-verification, theorem-certificate and portability controls where applicable.
- [ ] Distinguish a false *broad overclaim* from an established predecessor whose hypotheses were changed. No solver failure, imposed sign, moving welfare baseline, or changed selection passed off as reversal.
- [ ] Preserve genuine non-reversal, robust-invariance, impossibility and negative findings, not only surprising outcomes.

## D. Re-kill and skeptical editor (Stages 6/11)

- [ ] Strip sector/region labels, attempt exact theorem-absorption mappings, canonical-class reductions and general comparative-static/implementation theorems.
- [ ] Search later counterexamples and unpublished/published versions, appendices and same-author papers at the recorded cutoff.
- [ ] Test whether an attractive sign was engineered via special functional form, chosen parameters, impossible equilibrium branch, payoff sign or shifted welfare objective.
- [ ] State what a specialist newly learns **beyond the strongest predecessor**, preferably a condition, theorem, characterization, impossible-to-implement region or new robustness boundary rather than the rhetorical surprise alone.
- [ ] Retain current-paper verdict independently of prospective next ideas; future papers require author-selected objectives and Stage 0/2/3 re-entry.
- [ ] Document exact evidence, source depth, verifier, strongest skeptical-editor objection and stop rule.

## Compact result card

- Prior paper/theorem and exactly proved claim:
- Exact original domain/equilibrium/welfare comparison:
- Minimal economically motivated changed assumption (or unchanged-model theorem):
- Nested predecessor recovery:
- Proposed falsifiable reversal, impossibility, robust invariance or boundary:
- Closest general parent theorem and later-work resolution:
- Independent proof/counterexample/check; parameter-region scope:
- Specialist learning, editorial importance, survival limitations:
- Maturity, verdict, next stage and finite next kill test:

**Guardrail:** Do not demand paradox for its own sake. Do not relax the mathematical, prior-art, portability or field-editor significance gates. The original question may be maintained.
