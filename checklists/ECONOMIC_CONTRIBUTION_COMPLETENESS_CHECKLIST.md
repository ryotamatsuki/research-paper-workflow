# Economic Contribution, Question Completeness & Editorial Value Checklist

> Canonical authority: `GOVERNANCE.md` → `THEORY_PAPER_RESEARCH_PIPELINE.md` → Stage templates → this operational checklist. Proposed v2.9 minor refinement (2026-10-10). This is **not an additional numbered Stage**.

## Objective and proportionality

Separately evaluate **correctness**, **theorem/model novelty**, **economic importance**, **completeness relative to the claimed research question**, and **field-editor publication rationale**. A mathematical or Lean PASS, precise equilibrium characterization, and a distinct theorem do not, alone, prove that the paper asks/answers a consequential question.

This is a **context-sensitive test**, not a demand for an empirical dataset, a policy intervention, a larger model, an endogenous budget or a broader theorem. A self-contained pure-theory result, a substantial correction, or a deliberately diagnostic/negative result may be a full paper when its economic/scientific significance is defensible.

## When to run

- **Stage 3/4 (draft only):** one-sentence anticipated economic consequence and the strongest unanswered-question risk in the contribution memo.
- **Stage 6 (provisional after novelty re-kill):** complete Sections A–D with actual certified results and nearest prior work.
- **Stage 7 (welfare/institution):** complete Section E when policy facing; update significance and risk evidence.
- **Stage 7.5 (mandatory GO precondition):** an independent reviewer/editor challenge closes Sections A–G before the full-theory freeze decision.
- **Stage 8:** freeze the decision, scope/limitations and evidence links with the adopted claims.
- **Stage 11:** attack the frozen conclusions independently; classify any early omission as `CONTRIBUTION COMPLETENESS REGRESSION`.
- **Stage 12:** compare the survived contribution to *actual* journal publishing expectations (not desired prestige) and articulate a targeted desk objection.
- **After an editorial decision:** preserve original message and map objections to earliest responsible Stage, without rewriting old decisions or assuming a new extension would be accepted.

## A. Research-question coverage

| Promised question or claim (title, abstract, introduction) | Result that answers it (theorem/source) | Missing but material question | Classification | Evidence/justification |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| ... | ... | ... | `ESSENTIAL FOLLOW-UP / BOUNDED LIMITATION / FUTURE PAPER / NOT APPLICABLE` | ... |

An `ESSENTIAL FOLLOW-UP` is an omission that materially undermines the **stated** research question or the claimed field contribution; it cannot be closed by declaring it "outside scope" without explaining why the remaining contribution is independently sufficient.

## B. Economic significance, distinct from mathematics

- [ ] State the actual causal/strategic mechanism and new economic insight in plain language.
- [ ] Identify who acts, what decision or allocation changes, and whether welfare, incentives, organization, equilibrium selection, or measurement is meaningfully affected.
- [ ] Compare the substantive conclusion to the **strongest closest theorem**, not merely the application label or different notation.
- [ ] Distinguish a mathematically exact threshold/wedge from a practically or scientifically important phenomenon; explicitly justify why the exactness matters.
- [ ] Identify which assumptions are critical and whether policy/empirical interpretation is supported.
- [ ] For corrections/reassessments, show the corrected source claim and the *economic consequence*; a new mechanism is not mandatory.

**Editor-facing pitch (120–200 words or equivalent):** Why should this field publish the result even if the mathematics is sound and the closest-paper novelty audit passes? Name the relevant audience and what readers learn that changes their understanding.

**Strongest skeptical rejection paragraph (100–150 words):** State the economic weakness honestly and identify the missing contribution or institutional bridge.

## C. Scope and essential unanswered questions

- [ ] Test *paper promise vs results*, not a universal checklist of features every paper must contain.
- [ ] For each essential unanswered question identify the earliest appropriate Stage and the single diagnosed issue.
- [ ] Classify implementation/empirics/generality not needed for the claimed contribution as `BOUNDED LIMITATION` or `FUTURE PAPER` **with positive reasoning**, not as an automatic PASS.
- [ ] Classify unfamiliar-but-interesting possible extensions as optional, and preserve legitimate minimal theory.
- [ ] Reject unsupported phrases such as "centralization is optimal" when only a constrained planner comparison is solved.

## D. Bounded model-choice counterfactual (when a material gap exists)

| Research path | Distinct economic result expected or already certified | Closest parent theorem / absorption risk | New assumptions and empirical/institutional feasibility | Proof, novelty, and implementation costs | Decision |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `BASELINE ONLY` | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| `ONE MINIMAL MEANINGFUL EXTENSION` | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |
| `REFRAME / RESEARCH NOTE / PIVOT` | ... | ... | ... | ... | ... |

- [ ] Do not add a player, parameter, subsidy or data merely to make a model larger or chase a journal.
- [ ] Reject extension if it only restates a known implementation result in new notation.
- [ ] Prefer a bounded, independently falsifiable extension producing a distinct economic answer; preserve negative findings.
- [ ] Set a stop rule and one authorized next change. A new payoff/contract/timing requires Stage 3/4/4A/6 and affected later gates, not a Stage-7.5 prose patch.

## E. Conditional policy-facing audit

Complete **only when the paper's question or contribution is policy-facing**.

| Layer | Exact claim and modeled evidence | Status (`MODELED / DERIVED NOT IMPLEMENTED / DISCUSSED / OUT OF SCOPE`) | Remaining issue |
|---|---|---|---|
| **WHY**: decentralized distortion | ... | ... | ... |
| **SHOULD**: specified welfare objective and coordinated benchmark | ... | ... | ... |
| **COULD**: admissible instrument, participation/incentives, budget incidence and finance, authority, contractibility, information and enforcement | ... | ... | ... |

- [ ] A modeled planner optimum is not an implementable policy mechanism.
- [ ] No actionable recommendation is made with an absent instrument or unmodeled finance.
- [ ] Distinguish real-resource costs from transfers; preserve welfare-accounting consistency.
- [ ] If COULD remains outside scope, record a robust diagnosis-only editorial case and comparable field literature; if none, classify the gap as `ESSENTIAL FOLLOW-UP`.
- [ ] Primary institutional sources are used and descriptive programs are not asserted to instantiate a transfer game absent evidence.

## F. Independent skeptical-editor challenge

The reviewer must answer **without importing positive verdict labels** from the production team's math/novelty audit:

1. Why would a field editor accept this *rather than merely acknowledge it is correct*?
2. What is the highest-stakes unanswered question in the motivating problem?
3. What model/theorem/institutional evidence distinguishes this result from classic or generic externalities and welfare wedges?
4. If policy-facing, is there a defensible WHY–SHOULD–COULD story, or an explicitly justified diagnostic-only paper?
5. Is the preferred baseline-only/extension/note route chosen for research value rather than sunk cost or prestige?

Log reviewer identity/method and input exposure, exact objection, cited evidence, author resolution and residual uncertainty. A second AI opinion alone is not independent validation of source facts or theorem novelty.

## G. Stage 7.5 closeout and routing

- [ ] Stage-4A mathematical certificate exists; Stage-6 novelty/theorem absorption is closed.
- [ ] Editor-facing value case and skeptical refusal case both exist.
- [ ] All paper-promise gaps classified with evidence.
- [ ] Any policy-focused WHY–SHOULD–COULD gap is resolved or explicitly bounded with defensible standalone contribution.
- [ ] Bounded baseline / extension / reframe comparison completed *when material*, without uncontrolled feature growth.
- [ ] No unresolved `ESSENTIAL FOLLOW-UP` undermines a full-paper `GO`.
- [ ] Required model change is assigned to earliest Stage; original Theory Freeze not silently amended.
- [ ] Author sign-off is authentic and the verification actors are distinguished.

**Decision** (use only canonical Stage 7.5 routing):

- `GO`: contribution independently warrants full-paper investment, all essential gaps resolved, then go to Stage 7.5A (not Stage 8).
- `CONDITIONAL GO`: precisely one named completeness gap can be investigated through an authorized earlier-stage repair; affected stages must be repeated.
- `NO-GO / RESEARCH NOTE / PIVOT`: contribution is valid but insufficient for the claimed full-paper route.

Record date, version/SHA, artifact links, objection/response and stop rule. Unknowns remain `UNRESOLVED`; passing this audit is **not** a promise of peer review or publication.
