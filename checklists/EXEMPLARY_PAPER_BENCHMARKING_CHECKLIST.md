# Exemplary Paper Benchmarking & Synthesis Checklist

Use before Stage 10B (Stage 10A gate), at Stage 11 actual-manuscript attack, Stage 12 target calibration, Stage 13 retest, and Stage 14 final-PDF verification. Complement—not replace—EDITORIAL_ARGUMENT_QUALITY, EXPOSITION_STREAMLINING and REVIEWER_VERIFIABILITY.

## Search and selection
- [ ] Exact freeze/claims and Stage 9 GO; article type and research method; provisional venue if applicable.
- [ ] Bounded search across suitable venues and research traditions, record candidates and exclusions.
- [ ] Apply the acquisition filter prior to scoring/selection. **Full-text acquisition is a hard eligibility filter before scoring or selecting any writing exemplar.** Obtain the complete article and any appendices/supplements material to assessing its proofs or writing roles through lawful accessible sources (publisher, author/institutional repository, or legitimately supplied copy). An identifiable author accepted manuscript is acceptable when sufficiently complete and faithful to the published article; record its version and discrepancies. A PDF or complete HTML/text accessed and read through a supported tool is equally acceptable; local PDF download is not mandatory. Verify actual content access and completeness (first/last page or section, section/appendix inventory, no material gaps), then read the complete text including material proof sections and exhibits. A link, abstract, search snippets, AI summary, partial preview or an untested PDF URL is not acquisition. Log source, access date, version, coverage and locators; retain a lawful copy only where permitted, never republish copyrighted PDFs by default. If acquisition fails, mark the candidate EXCLUDED — FULL TEXT UNAVAILABLE, record why, and immediately advance to the next suitable candidate within a bounded search; do not score it or retain it as a selected exemplar, and do not hold the gate open merely to obtain a preferred famous paper. Require a fully acquired/read exemplar set with complete verified coverage of essential writing roles; no overall primary paper is mandatory; fewer than 3 papers permitted with a documented bounded-search justification. Only if no eligible set covers essential roles after reasonable substitution may a material blocker prevent GO. This filter applies to writing exemplars only; decisive inaccessible prior art remains subject to the separate fail-closed novelty/source-audit rules.
- [ ] Record FULL TEXT ACQUIRED / EXCLUDED — FULL TEXT UNAVAILABLE and COMPLETE / INCOMPLETE reading separately for each candidate; selected set contains only acquired and completely read sources. Verify all material proof appendices, exhibits and completeness. Do not defer unavailable preferred papers as blockers when a substitute covers the role.
- [ ] Default 3–5 selected papers assigned by writing role. Fewer (including one) only with justified search, comparison and full evidence coverage. An overall anchor is optional.
- [ ] Separate scientific proximity/novelty from writing merit; do not use prestige or citations alone and do not assert unanimous consensus.

## Rubric
Score each dimension 0 to its maximum using specific source evidence, not invented impressions. Near-zero = poor/irrelevant, midrange = useful but incomplete, high = directly suitable and exemplary; mark unaudited candidates UNVERIFIED rather than scoring from metadata. No mechanical threshold certifies a paper.
| Dimension | Max | Evidence questions |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| Topic, method, article-type fit | 20 | Same sort of economic inference, proofs, specialist audience? |
| Overall architecture | 15 | Puzzle, model, results and interpretation sequenced purposefully? |
| Prose clarity and economy | 15 | Precise terms, concise transitions, little redundant exposition? |
| Proof elegance and verifiability | 15 | Natural objects/lemmas, minimal steps, assumptions and bridges visible, proof burden allocated appropriately? |
| Economic intuition | 15 | Mechanisms and restrictions interpreted close to results; why not merely what? |
| Contribution framing | 10 | Distinct finding, inherited work, scope and limits explicit? |
| Exhibits and examples | 10 | Illustrations reveal structure; readable, evidence-grounded, not decorative? |
| Total | 100 | Record rationale and uncertainty per dimension. |

For an article type without relevant formal proof or exhibit, record reason; assess analytical transparency or illustrative economy as appropriate within the dimension, do not silently penalize article type or invent content. Report any genuinely N/A dimension and compare normalized applicable ratings explicitly; raw and normalized scores are qualitative judgments, not independent scientific evidence.

## Role-wise benchmarking, synthesis and coherence audit (mandatory)

The unit of selection is the writing technique, not the whole paper. Normally use complementary strengths from several eligible full-text exemplars; an overall anchor paper is optional. Total rubric scores inform screening only: do not mechanically choose the highest total or highest subscore, or assume a top-journal style fits every article type. For each applicable role (overall structure, Introduction and problem framing, clarity and prose economy, notation/model and theorem architecture, proof strategy and bridge visibility, economic intuition and post-result interpretation, novelty/contribution framing, figures/tables/examples, Discussion/conclusion and limitations), compare specific source-located techniques and choose the best fit for the frozen contribution. Multiple sources may support a single role; one source may support several roles. Document alternatives rejected, comparative reasons, limitations, and why a role is inapplicable if relevant. The preferred outcome is an original synthesis that can improve on individual exemplars where justified, not a claim of demonstrated superiority or imitation of source prose.

Include a role-selection matrix in EDITORIAL_EXEMPLAR_MAP.md (role; eligible candidates and exact locations; observed strengths/tradeoffs; selected move(s); project-specific adaptation; rejected alternatives and reasons; scientific guardrails). Integrate all adopted moves into MANUSCRIPT_BLUEPRINT.md. Before GO, explicitly audit whole-paper coherence: one economic narrative and contribution hierarchy; consistent notation, definitions, terminology and voice; compatible theorem/lemma and proof sequencing; no duplicated sections or intuition; figures serve the argument; main-text/appendix allocation preserves proof-critical bridges; transitions and limits preserve exact frozen claims, assumptions and negative results. Resolve conflicts between techniques by choosing an internally coherent article-specific architecture, not by copying or mechanically splicing sections. Document the conflict, alternatives, decision and justification; if no conflict, record tests and result. A single-paper solution requires evidence that alternative techniques were considered and it covers every material role without sacrificing fit. No unresolved material coherence or coverage defect may pass Stage 10A.

Stage 10B records exact implementation and justified deviations for each selected role. Stage 11 independently attacks both role-specific quality and the integrated paper's coherence after a cold read, not merely similarity to exemplars. Stage 12 recalibrates roles for the journal using the full-text filter; Stage 13 repairs and retests role quality and coherence; Stage 14 verifies the exact final PDF retains the resolved synthesis. Scientific defects return to the earliest scientific gate; never simplify a proof by silently changing its scope.

## Evidence and blueprint
- [ ] Original source-located moves for opening, section structure, notation/definitions, theorem hierarchy, proof-critical chains, post-result intuition, novelty boundary, figures/examples, discussion, limitations and conclusion. Record nontransferable features.
- [ ] Each claimed major result maps to frozen theorem/assumptions and narrative role, necessary bridges, proof location, explanatory device and caveats.
- [ ] Manuscript Blueprint defines section roles, reader-arrival targets, proof strategy, figure/table plan, cold-reader contract and Stage 12 refresh.
- [ ] No source prose copying, stylistic impersonation, borrowed theorem assumptions/conclusions, scope inflation, or scientific alterations to imitate exemplars. Missing or changed scientific content reopens earliest relevant gate.
- [ ] GO only with evidence-backed full coverage and blueprint; conditional/NO-GO blocks writing. No retrospectively fabricated benchmark after drafting.

## Downstream tests
- [ ] 10B follows blueprint, logs specific implementation and justified deviations, compiles/validates using existing gates.
- [ ] 11 cold-reads actual draft without writer explanation/blueprint, then compares each applicable dimension with source/draft locators, strongest communication-only rejection, severity, repair contract and retest. An unresolved material defect blocks GO. AI critique is not human review.
- [ ] 12 assesses selected-journal/article-type comparators and updates roles without changing science.
- [ ] 13 supplies before/after evidence for deficiencies, reruns critical read and preserves all scientific guardrails.
- [ ] 14 confirms exact final PDF and source SHA retain blueprint/adoption/referee/revision evidence. Missing benchmark → 10A; draft defect → 10B/13; scientific defect → relevant scientific gate.

Record PASS/REVISE/UNRESOLVED/NOT APPLICABLE — REASON per applicable subtest. Preserve exact links and file paths, reading depth, evaluator/evidence provenance and verdict. A high score, clean build, checklist tick or generically positive review cannot close a material defect.
