# Premature Novelty Claim Prevention Checklist

Status: unreleased post-v2.9 MINOR-class refinement; applies **inside** existing Stages 0/2/3/6. It is not a new Stage, a guarantee of originality, or a substitute for Stage-4/4A/6 verification.

## A. Stage 0 — register a falsifiable claim rather than a discovery

- [ ] State the motivating phenomenon separately from the proposed mechanism.
- [ ] Identify the **unit of claimed novelty**: theorem, whole game, generalization, correction, or application.
- [ ] Name at least one dangerous generic parent model or theorem family (not only a same-industry citation).
- [ ] Record concrete closest source(s), version/date and read depth: `FULL MODEL + PROOF`, `MODEL/RESULT ONLY`, `ABSTRACT ONLY`, or `UNAVAILABLE`.
- [ ] State an economically substantive predicted difference, the strongest fatal objection, and the first cheap falsifier.
- [ ] Do not write "independent theoretical discovery" or "novelty established".

## B. Stage 2 — provisional pre-model absorption screen

The model is not solved yet. This is a **cheap hypothesis-level** reduction, not proof of absence from literature.

- [ ] Rewrite the mechanism without application-specific entities; describe players, timing/commitment, payoff signs, strategies, constraints and information that are *actually supported*.
- [ ] Identify plausible general parent classes: monopsony/procurement and supplier outside options; Coase and durable-goods time inconsistency; static quality/product design; standard matching/search; mechanism design, bargaining, platform or oligopoly as appropriate.
- [ ] Attempt a direct relabeling/normalization/variable-elimination argument or short parent-theorem corollary before proposing model construction.
- [ ] For each decisive prior paper, record exact theorem/proposition/page/appendix when accessible. If not, record the missing source and consequence rather than guessing.
- [ ] Identify whether `ABSORBED`, `APPARENTLY DISTINCT`, or `UNRESOLVED` is supported by evidence; explicitly distinguish a *new strategic interaction* from merely known ingredients combined.
- [ ] State whether an apparent difference is primarily a mathematical result, a whole-game contribution, an empirical insight, or application interpretation.
- [ ] Do not equate a keyword gap, a solved numeric example, a named new institution or a surprising sign with originality.
- [ ] Preserve one material blocker under `CONDITIONAL GO` only when the narrowly bounded contract is valid under canonical rules. Do not award Stage-2 `GO` for an unclosed decisive novelty problem.
- [ ] Pass the parent-theorem mapping and strongest objection into Stage 3 and Stage 4.

## C. Stage 3 — keep maturity labels independent

For each candidate record separately:

**MATHEMATICS**: `HYPOTHESIS` / `COMPUTATION-ONLY` / `MATHEMATICALLY VERIFIED` (only where exact evidence exists).

**NOVELTY**: `UNSCREENED` / `PRE-SCREEN SURVIVED` / `UNRESOLVED` / `ABSORBED`.

- [ ] The candidate is not called an "independent discovery" merely because a model, code, or closed form exists.
- [ ] Where prior art absorbs one proposed result, other genuinely distinct claims may still be assessed rather than automatically discarded.
- [ ] Every accepted Stage-3 candidate has an explicit minimal Stage-4 test and failure boundary.

## D. Stage 6 — actual theorem-level closure

- [ ] Replace the schematic pre-model model with the actual Stage-4/4A-certified canonical model and exact proposition.
- [ ] Re-open strongest source models/proofs and forward papers. Attempt exact variable/parameter mapping and direct corollary derivation; record precise limitations.
- [ ] Classify `ABSORBED` / `PARTIALLY ABSORBED` / `NOT ABSORBED IN VERIFIED SCOPE` / `UNRESOLVED`.
- [ ] Only a substantiated `NOT ABSORBED IN VERIFIED SCOPE` may be described as `EVIDENCE-SCOPED NOVELTY CERTIFIED`, with surveyed date/cutoff, source versions, exact claim and source-access limitations. Not a priority guarantee.
- [ ] The mathematical certificate and novelty assessment remain separate.
- [ ] If an earlier confident novelty claim fails, record a **false-positive novelty regression**, identify missed parent class/reading gap, and correct current downstream claims.

## E. Regression fixture

Before approving a relevant workflow change or completing a difficult Stage-2/6 novelty report, exercise the manual cases in `examples/PREMATURE_NOVELTY_REGRESSION.md`. The applied-obsolescence case must never pass on the strength of a solved payoff formula alone; the component-overlap case must not be summarily rejected without a whole-game test.
