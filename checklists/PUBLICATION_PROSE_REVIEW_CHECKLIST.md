# Stage 13P — Independent Publication Prose & Exemplary-Paper Review Checklist

> Prospective mandatory v4.0 Stage 13P. Use with `templates/STAGE_13P_INDEPENDENT_PROSE_REVIEW.md`, `EDITORIAL_ARGUMENT_QUALITY_CHECKLIST.md`, the Stage 10A complete-exemplar register and the Stage 12 journal refresh. **A tick alone is never evidence.**

## A. Source snapshot and reviewer independence
- [ ] Entry Stage 13 **GO**; exact integrated source SHA, PDF hash/page count and all Appendix/supplement variants fixed
- [ ] Phase-A reviewer sees only the complete manuscript PDF and neutral target/article type; no benchmark map, author intention, previous review, fix log or repo history
- [ ] Prior model/agent exposure and inability to blind **honestly disclosed**; AI review never described as human peer review
- [ ] Phase-A full-reading coverage includes Abstract–Conclusion, captions, tables, figures, references, Appendix, Code/Data/AI/other Declarations
- [ ] Phase-A report recorded/committed before Stage 10A/12 exemplar information was exposed

## B. Phase A: blind publication prose review
- [ ] Referee independently paraphrases problem, importance, main result, fixed vs varied objects, mechanism and limitations, without an answer key
- [ ] Opening communicates an economic question before unnecessary machinery
- [ ] Natural idiomatic economics-journal English, not polished filler or artificially complex vocabulary
- [ ] Paragraphs have distinct purposes; transitions advance the argument without repeated throat-clearing
- [ ] Nearby explanation of results states the opposing forces and intuition, rather than just algebra
- [ ] Contribution speaks through a meaningful comparison rather than self-praise, self-deprecation or publisher-addressed defensiveness
- [ ] Source credit is fair, accurate, located, and not a formulaic author-by-author catalogue
- [ ] No avoidable repetitions across Abstract, Introduction, Results, Discussion and Conclusion
- [ ] Valid theorem restrictions, counterexamples and negative results remain explicit, not removed as “weak wording”
- [ ] No author workflow residue (Stage-4A/11, hostile audit, PR/issue log, CI results, regression-test narration) in the scientific story
- [ ] Economic game's “Stage 1”, legitimate reproducible computer-assisted proof and necessary Code statement **not mistakenly prohibited**
- [ ] Appendix proofs, Figure captions/notes and funding/conflict/AI/data/code statements read as manuscript prose, not skipped
- [ ] Strongest communication-only desk-reject paragraph grounded in verbatim short source passages produced even for otherwise favorable draft
- [ ] Every MAJOR/FATAL item gives page/paragraph, short original quote, defect, concrete repair, and claim-scope guardrail

## C. Phase B: real exemplar comparison
- [ ] Exemplar source access is complete and documented (author manuscript or publication, actual full text + material appendix); never abstract/snippets/untested URLs
- [ ] Stage 12 target-journal/article-type comparators refreshed without prestige bias
- [ ] For each relevant role, give precise exemplar **source/version/page/section/paragraph** and manuscript locator
- [ ] Compare opening puzzle, topic sentences/transitions, explanation economy, natural English and section flow
- [ ] Compare model notation, theorem-prose bridge, economic intuition, figure/table commentary and proof exposition
- [ ] Compare contribution framing, literature positioning, Discussion/limitations, final reader takeaway and whole-paper coherence
- [ ] Explain *why* each exemplar technique helps this paper, and when it is inappropriate
- [ ] Each role rated `STRONG / ADEQUATE / WEAK / NOT APPLICABLE — reason` with observed prose; no unsupported numerical index
- [ ] No copying, imitation of author voice, source-model import, mechanistic template mimicry or long unlicensed quotations
- [ ] Any `WEAK` material role has bounded passage-specific correction and an independent retest plan

## D. Repairs, second reader and gate
- [ ] Freeze Phase A before Phase B; never rewrite the blind assessment retrospectively
- [ ] Unified issues have unique IDs, severity, page/short quote, owner, resolution condition and evidence
- [ ] Text edits have before/after proofs and no new mathematical, novelty, causal or welfare claims
- [ ] Scientific restrictions, negative results and proof-critical bridges preserved; issues that require changing substance routed upstream
- [ ] Second **independent cold reader** sees revised full manuscript, not the solution key; actually confirms material closures
- [ ] `PROSE_REFEREE_REPORT.md`, `EXEMPLAR_PROSE_COMPARISON.md`, `PROSE_ISSUE_REGISTER.md`, `PROSE_REVISION_AUDIT.md`, `STAGE13P_GATE_REPORT.md` (or exact equivalents) retained
- [ ] `GO` requires no unresolved material issue or unexplained WEAK role; `CONDITIONAL GO` with one bounded fix **blocks Stage 14**; `NO-GO` returns to 13/10A or earliest substantive Stage
- [ ] Final Stage13P GO records immutable accepted source SHA and PDF hash
- [ ] Stage14 actual **submission PDF** and all text variants reconciled against GO snapshot
- [ ] Every post-GO textual change triggers targeted or full independent re-review according to impact, including captions/appendix/declarations
- [ ] Final author scientific responsibility, AI disclosure and portal attestations separately verified; never infer from a model/AI/CI check

## Explicit fail examples

A build passing is not a prose GO. Internal “Stage-4A certification” in article narrative, self-proclaimed novelty worthiness, unlocated benchmark praise, inaccessible published-paper examples, changed theorem quantifier disguised as cleanup, and a post-GO manuscript rewrite not rechecked each block a full Stage13P PASS.
