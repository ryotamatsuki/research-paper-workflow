# Calibration report — 2026-11-16

## Observed defect
In v5.0 gate.py, all decisive sources had to be adequate at model/result depth, all eight search lanes complete (except justified L1 NA), all residual/parent/red-team/removal fields filled, and no material blocker before any STANDARD handoff. EPOCH added criteria only after this shared hard gate. The former protocol's full deep-screen threshold was therefore also the research-initiation threshold. It is not established that any of the rejected research claims was actually novel.

v5.1 creates a lower, evidence-bearing STANDARD research-investment gate (PROMISING, novelty UNRESOLVED) alongside the full prescreen, and keeps a distinct EPOCH queue. A directly threatening unverified claim is still HOLD. Existing parent classes do not by themselves imply absorption. Rejection of an exact claim never automatically rejects all possible extensions.

## Prospective evaluation design (not run)
Create a held-out benchmark of published theoretical papers spanning specialist, field and general journals (at least 4 per meaningful contribution type if feasible), plus matched absorbed claims, unresolved near-misses, and good-faith negative results. Establish first public version dates including working papers. Provide motivation only, mask titles/answers, restrict retrieval to versioned materials available before the cutoff; exclude answer versions, later references that reveal answers and track leakage. LLM memorization remains a possible confound: do not call this truly blind. Include contemporary unknown-answer tasks reviewed by economists independently. Keep reviewer assessment of researchability separate from whether a result eventually proves new. Compare v5.0 and v5.1 with same tasks, models, budgets, source snapshot and counterbalanced order; adjudicators see neither version nor labels where possible. Score candidate-level precision of eligible recommendations, recall of defensible questions, fatal-overlap admissions, evidence quality, search/inspection cost and recycling improvement; report uncertainty and disagreements, not fixed admission quotas. Prospective evidence required to claim actual improvement. No guaranteed epoch discovery or journal acceptance.
