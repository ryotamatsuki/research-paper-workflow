# TDF v5.1 — Loop Protocol

## 1. Authority and purpose
Canonical GOVERNANCE, THEORY_PAPER_RESEARCH_PIPELINE, stage templates and checklists take precedence. This optional pre-Stage-0 framework does not alter Stage verdicts, proof, novelty or freeze obligations. v4 question-first principles and L2 model-construction catalog remain available. v5.0 reference protocol in validation/ is historical, not controlling. No unpublished research enters a public repository by default.

## 2. Two distinct generators
STANDARD searches vertically: select a relevant predecessor; identify a solved result, scope and economically justified boundary; generate a falsifiable new result, institutional comparison, generalization, correction, new mechanism or substantive application. For a new result in a known class, test whether it is already a direct corollary, not whether the class is familiar. An application must change a real economic understanding or policy ranking, not only rename an actor. The target is a researchable contribution in a suitable specialist journal, not guaranteed acceptance. No requirement to establish public provision as uniquely necessary: a comparative domain theorem may suffice. Separate comparative advantage from exclusive necessity.

EPOCH searches horizontally and deeply: isolate assumptions/units or equivalences, find new discrimination or unification, compare at least two settings (hypothetical allowed if labelled), explain why the proposed concept is indispensable relative to strongest parent theories. A second context or unexpected sign alone does not suffice. A promising standard idea is not an epoch discovery.

Run BOTH as distinct queues with separate allocations and outputs. Share verified bibliography/query ledger; charge actual new searches once, record reuse with original ID. Do not count one evaluation as two independent reviews. Initial default overall budget: rounds 3 per track, total candidates 12, deep screens 6, searches 60, inspections 30 (single mode uses full budget); BOTH starts with 7 candidates/4 deep screens/36 searches/18 inspections STANDARD and 5/2/24/12 EPOCH, reallocate unused quotas explicitly while preserving total and at least one recycling round per active track. The numerical allocations are operational defaults, not pass quotas. Log actual per-track rounds and shared counters; the optional Budget object supports the single aggregate cap (BOTH uses rounds cap 6). Resume never discards prior usage. Explicit author authorization for aggregate limit increases.

## 3. State machine
INTAKE → separate STANDARD/EPOCH frontier and generation queues → DEDUP → QUICK_SCREEN → DECIDE.
At DECIDE: (a) DISCOVERY/HOLD with missing test: record and prioritize targeted evidence or continue other candidates; (b) REJECT: exact claim and evidence → RECYCLE → successor with new contrast/falsifier, same lineage; (c) PROMISING STANDARD: eligible for author selection, optional DEEP_SCREEN; (d) PROMISING EPOCH: HOLD pending deeper evidence; (e) PRE_SCREEN_SURVIVED: eligible for selection. Use the remaining budget to compare tracks; do not let a standard success silently terminate requested EPOCH work. No candidate → bounded continuation, checkpoint budget stop, access hold, or exhausted in stated scope. At stop report eligible candidates even if budget exhausted. No automatic Stage 0. New source/claim may reopen a rejected candidate; record new evidence ref and explicit change. Semantic duplicate review remains human/AI task; code checks exact normalized signature only.

## 4. Evidence tiers
Use L1 institutional/topic; L2 abstract strategic structure; L3 claims and opposing results; L4 general parent; L5 adjacent disciplines; L6 backward citations; L7 forward citations/author neighbors; L8 frontier/working papers. For PROMISING require actual targeted L2/L4/L8 evidence, closest primary-source comparison (abstract permissible only for hypothesis-level triage), precise question/claim/contrast/falsifier, benchmark, distinct delta, economic motive, contribution route, bounded next test. Lanes may be SCOPED for quick screen; record limits. No assertion of absence of relevant literature. Missing source that could directly settle the claim is FATAL_UNRESOLVED until examined; less decisive proof/extension or additional coverage may be AUDITABLE if justified and bounded. Every serious objection is categorized with evidence and next test; each inadequate decisive source must be explicitly linked to an AUDITABLE or FATAL_UNRESOLVED objection; REFUTED requires evidence. Do not mark a decisive source adequate merely to force eligibility. When uncertainty about overlap concerns the whole candidate and no identified residual survives, HOLD, not PROMISING.

For full prescreen use all eight lanes with query evidence (L1 NA only for justified noninstitutional topic); record exact model/result comparison and adequate reading depth for decisive sources, counterfactual removal and red-team attack; no outstanding objections. Use canonical Stage 2 closest paper matrix, forward/backward searches, same author, version dedup, theorem domain and limitation register. No exact match is not positive evidence. EPOCH adds necessity, contrast and two settings. If no actual independent reviewer exists, say SELF_RED_TEAM and specify shared context. A later Stage 2 can reject even PRE_SCREEN_SURVIVED.

## 5. Distinct axes and claim-level decisions
- Maturity DISCOVERY / PROMISING / PRE_SCREEN_SURVIVED; disposition ACTIVE / HOLD / REJECT / MERGED; novelty UNSCREENED / UNRESOLVED / ABSORBED / PRE-SCREEN SURVIVED; mathematical status HYPOTHESIS / COMPUTATION-ONLY / MATHEMATICALLY VERIFIED (exact scope only); economic relevance and evidence gaps separately.
- Absorption requires primary model/proposition mapping at sufficient depth, not title similarity. Reject-no-residual requires reasoned evidence; do not infer that a broad theme is exhausted. Missing evidence is not absorption. A strong relevant but inaccessible paper is a HOLD if decisive. Known components + potential new complete-game implication may remain eligible in STANDARD, if credible comparison and test survive quick review. No positive quota, no unsupported novelty labels.
- PROMISING means permission to consider an audit investment, not probability of publication. Standard may hand off on author selection; epoch requires full prescreen. Stage 0 makes its own decision and does not inherit GO. Stage 2 and 6 remain independent formal checks; Stage 4A etc unchanged.

## 6. Recycling discipline
On rejection record exact failed claim, source/version, theorem scope, mapping or other reason. Look for a same-question/new-result boundary, conflicting results on common benchmark, policy instrument comparison or a genuinely different question. Prefer one targeted predecessor deep dive before hopping themes in STANDARD. Branch if evidence supports it, not as compulsory output. Record successor parent ID, why distinct, original claim still rejected, fresh search/objections; no circular reopening without new evidence. If no defensible residual, retire branch. Maintain uncertainty classes and record actual candidate/query/inspection operations; never synthesize an audit trail retrospectively.

## 7. Output and handoff
Mode-specific candidate ledger, search ledger, source comparison, rejection/gap/successor lineage, resource usage, remaining tasks and stop reason. For up to 3 eligible candidates per track, provide plausible contribution, strongest threat, exact audit plan and caveats; researcher selects. The author can directly enter Stage 0 under canonical workflow independently of TDF, but not relabel a blocked TDF candidate as passed. Record provenance and use templates/Stage0_Handoff.md. Keep private research observations and detailed case fixtures private.

## 8. Calibration and change control
Test routing with positive, negative, boundary, duplicate and resume fixtures. Retrospective published-paper-inspired fixtures test operational calibration only. Prospective validation must use pre-first-public-version cutoff, remove answer paper and versions from retrieval, verify actual contemporaneous sources, use independent adjudication and log leakage (model prior training cannot be guaranteed blind). Compare v5.0/v5.1 with equal budgets, fixed topics, candidate-level labels from independent reviewers, report viable-question recall and absorbed-claim false admission plus cost. Neither counts nor passing unit tests prove better discovery. Retain the v5.0 comparison protocol and earlier operational baselines for reproducibility. Record material changes in subsequent versions.
