## Chronology

| Step | Question | Model / audit | Key finding | Verdict | Why next step was allowed |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Legacy paper | Can the old model itself be modernized? | Re-read the old vertical-channel setup | The economic question survived better than the inherited model; microfoundation, parameter interpretation, cross-regime comparability, and novelty required reconstruction | `NO-GO` for direct modernization | Preserve the question, not the legacy model |
| Static service-spillover model | Can local service explain dealer survival when another retailer sells the same brand? | Manufacturer `M`, local dealer `L`, mass retailer `R`; cross-channel service spillover | Under sales-margin-only financing, service satisfies `e* = (ell/k) q_L*`; hence `q_L → 0` implies `e → 0` | Publication baseline `NO-GO`; research question `CONDITIONAL GO` | The failure identified one missing margin: sales-independent service financing |
| Static service contract | Can a separate service contract support `q_L = 0, e > 0`? | Add only sales-independent service compensation | Feasibility is restored, but the service-only former dealer is economically equivalent to a generic service contractor in the static model | Historical Stage 2 `NO-GO` | The next question becomes why former-dealer identity should matter |
| Installed base only | Does durable-goods history make the former dealer special? | Add brand-level installed base `I_t` | Past sales can create current service demand, but a provider-independent installed base does not distinguish former dealer from third party | `INSUFFICIENT` | The missing object is provider-specific inherited capability, not merely service demand |
| Legacy service capital | Can past relationship-specific activity make provider identity payoff-relevant? | Add manufacturer-specific service capability `K_t` | Former dealer can differ from a fresh provider; candidate retail/service exit separation and dynamic vertical distortion appear | Historical Stage 3 `CONDITIONAL GO` | Continue only if the accumulation of `K_t` can be microfounded and independently reproduced |
