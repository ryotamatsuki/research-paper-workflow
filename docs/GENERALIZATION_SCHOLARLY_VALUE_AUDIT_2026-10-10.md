# Economic-theory generalization: scholarly contribution versus personal project fit
**2026-10-10. Source-driven correction to the previously overly categorical applied-theory NO-GO rule.** Journal pages checked directly; published article abstracts and TE full original article reviewed. This report is NOT an academic novelty certificate.

## Publisher and referee criteria
- Journal of Mathematical Economics: https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-mathematical-economics/0304-4068 — new/correct mathematics alone is NOT enough; it must have important real economic content, but that content can belong to ANY economics field. Formalization itself can reveal previously unseen economic ideas. Economic theory content includes general equilibrium and game/choice concepts; it does not need a particular municipal subsidy implication.
- Theoretical Economics (Econometric Society): https://ojs-es.econometricsociety.org/editorial.php — explicitly welcomes PURE and APPLIED theory, with substantial innovative theoretical contribution; focus and clarity relevant.
- Journal of Economic Theory: https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/journal-of-economic-theory/0022-0531 — original innovative theory in all economic areas.
- Econometrica: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14680262/homepage/aims.htm — abstracts and applications, theoretical and methodological innovation accepted.
- Journal of Public Economic Theory: https://www.accessecon.com/Pubs/JPET/default.aspx?linkID=442&menu=2&page=content — original correct rigorous theory, clear exposition and contribution; math complexity neither prerequisite nor defect.
- Journal of Regional Science: https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/page/journal/14679787/homepage/productinformation.html — theoretical and methodological articles can qualify with important geographic/urban/regional subject value; no local focus merely signals JRS fit, not general academic irrelevance.
- RSUE: https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/regional-science-and-urban-economics/0166-0462 — original theoretical/empirical research with substantial spatial-economic content.
- Economics Letters: https://shop.elsevier.com/journals/economics-letters/0165-1765 — important examples/counterexamples can be published without a completed general theorem, subject to novelty and significance.

## Actual published GENERALIZATION precedents (not all original full proofs independently audited)
1. Bhowmik & Yannelis (2024), "Equilibria in abstract economies with a continuum of agents with discontinuous and non-ordered preferences", *Journal of Mathematical Economics*. DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmateco.2024.103049 ; official original abstract https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/abs/pii/S0304406824001095 . Widens economic equilibrium existence from known finite-agent and continuum-preference setups to discontinuous, nonordered, interdependent and price-dependent preferences. Important foundational ECONOMIC theory, no immediate municipal industry policy required. Publisher abstract examined, full proof not audited.
2. Khan, McLean & Uyanik (2025), "Excess demand approach with non-convexity and discontinuity: a generalization of the Gale–Nikaido–Kuhn–Debreu lemma", *Economic Theory* 79:1167–1190, DOI https://doi.org/10.1007/s00199-025-01641-9 . Open full published journal article inspected. Weakens convexity and continuity and proves a Walrasian equilibrium existence result in settings with externalities and irregular preferences; economic theory contributions can be mathematical at core.
3. Gonczarowski, Kominers & Shorrer (2025), "To infinity and beyond: A general framework for scaling economic theories", *Theoretical Economics* 20(2):511–542, DOI https://doi.org/10.3982/TE5878 . Original publisher full HTML reviewed. Framework for strengthening finite-model statements to infinite cases using logical compactness, applicable to revealed preference and games. Significant methodology/theorem-domain contribution without municipal policy application.
4. Rehbeck (2024), "A menu dependent Luce model with a numeraire", *Journal of Mathematical Economics* 110:102920, DOI https://doi.org/10.1016/j.jmateco.2023.102920 . Publisher original abstract reviewed; generalizes discrete-choice representation and connects to choice overload, not municipal policy.
A real published example does not imply any superficially similar proposed generalization is novel or publishable.

## Proper distinction: academic merit, topic fit and evidence maturity
1. **SCHOLARLY THEORY VALUE**: judge what a result adds to economics models or methodology across ALL of economics, including broader applicability, weaker assumptions, existence/uniqueness/stability/identification/robustness, stronger comparative statics, new counterexamples, and cross-field unification. An outcome can be a legitimate economic-theory contribution WITHOUT changing the currently studied local subsidy ranking.
2. **CURRENT PROJECT FIT**: DIRECT (local industrial policy); ADJACENT (likely relevant with suitable argument); OUTSIDE (another economic theory topic). OUTSIDE -> **DEFER / SEPARATE PROJECT / ROUTE**, NOT a universal research-worth NO-GO.
3. **SOURCE AND PUBLICATION UNCERTAINTY**: UNPROVED theoretical conjecture vs solved original mathematical result; strongest theorem absorption risks; broad plausible audience JME/TE/JET vs location-focused JRS/RSUE vs Economics Letters. A strong theorem alone does not guarantee acceptance.
Technical feasibility and rigorous proof are necessary methodology/certification, not replacements for contribution.
If result is mathematical abstraction with NO relation even to economic theory, JME publisher says novel math alone is inadequate; separately assess mathematical merit if requested.

## Reclassification examples
| Hypothesis | Theory significance | Current local policy fit | Appropriate decision |
|---|---|---|---|
| Existence with genuinely weaker discontinuity/nonconvexity conditions for Walrasian equilibria | Potentially substantive mathematical economics (published precedents) | OUTSIDE or ADJACENT | Preserve as research candidate for JME/Economic Theory rather than NO-GO |
| General scaling from finite to infinite games and demand consistency | Potential general theory/methodology (TE precedent) | OUTSIDE | DEFER / SEPARATE theory track |
| The same industrial policy result under less unrealistic firm information constraints | Potential substantive generalization/robustness | DIRECT | bounded extension test if not known |
| Identical known theorem derived after variable relabeling | No novel result if mapped exactly | Any | NO-GO for originality claim |
| Mathematical topological result unrelated to economic constructs or predictions | Economic-journal contribution not established; could be pure mathematics | OUTSIDE | ROUTE to mathematics; not "worthless" |
| Novel numerical-looking threshold introduced mechanically by arbitrary rent share | Likely economically weak, regardless of exact formula | DIRECT | NO-GO for weak economic model, with explanation |

## Required governance adjustment
- Earlier applied track declared that if there is no new local-industrial-policy/equilibrium/welfare conclusion a new mathematical theorem is NO-GO. **CORRECT:** that may be OUTSIDE CURRENT PROJECT, but not necessarily without value in MATHEMATICAL ECONOMICS or applied theory in another field.
- Mathematical economics requires *economics-theory content*, broadly defined, not immediate policies or measurable welfare effects.
- During Stage0/2 provide **separate descriptive fields**: scholarly contribution route, economics domain and relevance, local policy project fit, level of novelty source support, likely journal family and exact next bounded analytical test.
- NO-GO at research-claim level: an identified existing theorem absorbs same result, trivial/cosmetic/tautological economic delta, false equilibrium or unsound premise. A misfit (other economics) gets DEFER/ROUTE; an undecided theorem can merit a bounded test under its appropriate discipline.
- Current theory-only preference does not require adding empirics, calibration, datasets or a welfare-optimal policy; applied theory can be explanatory or methodological.
- Stage6 strict proof+novelty and downstream journal editorial audit remain unchanged. No 0–100 mathematical/policy score.

**Audit verdict:** the user's criticism is correct. Official JME and TE standards, and 2024/2025 published results, demonstrate that foundational generality may be important economic scholarship outside a narrow policy subfield. Do not confuse project-fit with field-wide research value. Earlier policy is amended in draft workflow branch, not released.
