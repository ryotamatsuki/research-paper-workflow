## Core independent objection and correction
A checklist that merely asks whether 'green effects matter' risks two false answers: a theorem can be new and economically valuable when generic, and removing a primitive may delete the policy shock so the supposed counterfactual is not a comparable experiment. The revision therefore **separates** thematic causal role, theorem absorption and economic learning, and allows honest non-comparability or NOT RUN. Stage 3 predictions are not 'proved' until Stage 4–7.5. Stage 11 must independently attempt a reduction to the nearest general parent.

## Scenario-by-scenario review (distinct from executable classification test)
A — An unchanged general fiscal competition model with merely green nomenclature is absorbed; reject a **new environmental theory** claim, not every possible useful applied exposition. The label-only version fails even if its write-up is polished.
B — A real environmental friction (e.g. emissions-damage interaction/technical path dependence) changes a comparable theorem or welfare condition; if the matched M2 genuinely isolates it, ESSENTIAL/CONTRIBUTORY may be supported in its exact domain. A numerical example alone does not prove universal necessity.
C — A new general theorem under weaker assumptions can create substantial learning without environmental policy. Thematic-feature audit is NOT APPLICABLE; demand parent-theorem absorption evidence instead.
D — Existing pieces may generate a genuinely new interaction and sharp condition not in the parent theorem. Correct classification depends on the theorem comparison; 'all ingredients known' is not a rejection proof.

## Method and limitations of tests
The automated six-case classifier tests the correctness of **policy routing given stipulated input**. It does not solve M0/M1/M2 games, authenticate the economic value of every case, or replicate the paper's Lean/numeric certificates. The separate pinned-source verifier checks real Green file SHA/anchor text to prevent misattribution; it cannot establish the untested environmental counterfactual. This division is intentional. A future end-to-end research-agent exercise should take full mathematical papers as input, blind-review candidate models, and independently derive and falsify the claimed theorems; current CI must not be described as such.
