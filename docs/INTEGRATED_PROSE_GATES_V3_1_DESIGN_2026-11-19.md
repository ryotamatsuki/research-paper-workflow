# Decision: keep Stage 11 and integrate final prose into Stage 13 (v3.1 draft)

Date 2026-11-19; Draft PR #35 revision in place. Main/v3.0 are unchanged, no release.

## Why not a new Stage 13P?

Current v3.0 Stage 11 already mandates a source-located cold editorial referee and complete published-paper exemplars on the Stage-10B draft. A second *numbered* prose Stage duplicates it. The real gap is that later Stage-12 journal selection and Stage-13 rewriting can invalidate the draft-based assessment. We therefore strengthen **Stage 11 draft detection** and embed **independent source/PDF-bound final prose closure at the end of existing Stage 13**, checked by Stage 14/15.

## Evidence contract

Independent cold completed PDF full reading (Phase A) must be saved before source exemplars or prior notes are accessed; then fully read Stage-10A and Stage-12 comparator text is examined passage by passage (Phase B). Defects are repaired with theorem/negative-result safeguards; a second independent cold complete-paper review is compulsory. Stage-13 GO is blocked by unclosed material defects and missing evidence. Stage-14/15 invalidates stale prose approval after any manuscript text edit.

## Compatibility

No numbered stages, canonical GO meanings, normal route or stale-state logic change. Strengthening an existing Stage's evidence and all-page review requirement is MINOR under docs/VERSIONING_POLICY.md §§4–5, prospective v3.1, NOT v4.0. PR #35 is draft; v3.0 remains the published immutable release. Historical reports are not retroactively reclassified as Stage-13 prose GO.

## Pilot scope

The Green Subsidies source-only retrospective pilot found internal workflow residue and repeated defensive framing. It did not establish full PDF initial blindness, Stage-10A complete exemplar acquisition, or a second independent read of a revised manuscript. The structural CI validates contract links only. No paper should claim Stage-13 GO merely because these files exist.
