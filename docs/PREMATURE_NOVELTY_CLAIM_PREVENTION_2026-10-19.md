# Premature Novelty Claim Prevention — Unreleased Refinement

Date: 2026-10-19
Target: prospective v2.10 minor-class work on a feature branch (not a tag or release).
Current stable reference: v2.9.
Change type: **MINOR**, under `docs/VERSIONING_POLICY.md` §4.

## Trigger and diagnosis

A circular durable-goods research idea was initially characterized as an independently novel theoretical mechanism after closed-form derivation and numerical checking. The subsequent literature gate located nearby work on durability, buyback/remanufacturing, and time inconsistency; application-neutral rewriting also exposed a generic buyer/seller-outside-option structure. Earlier confidence exceeded what the original-theorem access and absorption evidence warranted.

The correction is not to require comprehensive literature omniscience before any algebra, nor to terminate all new combinations of known components. It is to keep hypotheses, mathematical correctness, prior-art screening, and evidence-scoped novelty certification clearly separate.

## Decision changes (without new Stage)

- Stage 0: record generic parent-theorem threat and a falsifiable economic difference.
- Stage 2: demand a **cheap, pre-model** literature-absorption attempt and explicit source-access limitation. An unsolved model cannot be certified by a fictitious full canonical derivation.
- Stage 3: carry separate maturity axes and forbid unqualified "independent discovery" language.
- Stage 6: certify (if warranted) only the **actual theorem**, scoped to actual read sources and cutoff. Record and correct false-positive claims.
- Maintain Stage 4's existing full canonical model and Stage 4A's independent mathematical review. Do not move Stage-6 proof absorption into Stage 2 or confuse tests.

## Evidence boundaries and important safeguards

An unavailable but decisive paper is `UNRESOLVED`, not an automatic proof of plagiarism or of originality. Known ingredients do not automatically absorb their full game. Stage 2 `GO` authorizes the next mathematical test, not a publication/novelty guarantee. `EVIDENCE-SCOPED NOVELTY CERTIFIED` is conditional on Stage 6 and retains literature scope limits. Historical v2.9 reports, tags, audits and release notes are not rewritten.
