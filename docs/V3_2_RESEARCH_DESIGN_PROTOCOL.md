# v3.2 — Research Question–Mechanism Alignment & Economic Model Design

Status: ACTIVE after author approval on 2026-11-19 and main merge; parent: v3.1 (main 11fad4e8ad60e22c8a8868957d60ed4c6f5b9851). No new canonical Stage, verdict or route. The rules below refine existing checks; Stage templates contain their local execution contracts. Governance remains controlling.

## Principle, two legitimate starting routes, and scope
The objective is incremental **economic learning**, not novelty by labels, added primitives or proof length. A theorem-first paper can make a substantial contribution without an application-specific primitive. A theme-first paper can have a valuable generally applicable result but must not market it as a theme-specific causal finding without evidence.
- THEME-FIRST: phenomenon/policy/institution/empirical regularity -> precise unanswered question -> mapped literature -> candidate friction -> predicted mechanism and falsifier -> minimum model -> claim-specific inference.
- MODEL-FIRST: mathematically identified anomaly/theorem/correction -> parent theorem/class and absorption map -> economically meaningful result -> candidate applications -> check that chosen application claims do not exceed actual identification.
- HYBRID: record both paths, their relative role and primary contribution. Reclassify at any Stage when warranted, update title/question/contribution classification and reopen affected Stage 0/2/3/4/6 and freeze certificates. Neither route is an academic ranking.
For corrections, generalizations, known-model new theorems, equilibrium characterization and important applications, no new sector-specific mechanism is required. Economic significance and claim scope still require evidence.

## Evidence protocol and cost control
Use the existing project research decision log and Stage reports; **equivalent named sections are acceptable**. Do not create duplicative forms where the same evidence is present with exact location, version and source. Pre-specified expectations and observed results must be visibly separated; failed and unrun tests must be labelled FAILED and NOT RUN, respectively. Missing evidence = UNRESOLVED, never automatically a negative finding. No prior GO is automatically inherited when the proposition, research question, application claim or proof scope changes.
Before algebra, pay for the cheapest discriminating comparison: application-name deletion, parent-theorem substitution, zero/hold-fixed thematic primitive, one valid alternative mechanism. Escalate to full numerical/global computation only when this screen leaves a substantive question. Choose benchmark and falsifier before looking at the desired result. Avoid adding a candidate simply to meet a count. Keep a bounded time/compute budget and stop when the classification is sufficient.

## Stage 0 — route and testable question
Record route, primary research question (one sentence), economic stakes, nearest parent paper/theorem, known versus unknown, distinct economic friction or theorem object, anticipated contribution route, predicted contradictory outcomes, falsifier, provisional source evidence, and first cheap comparison. THEME-FIRST must say where its distinctive feature could enter the payoff/choice/information/timing/constraint/externality/equilibrium. MODEL-FIRST must name the parent and potential economic learning. HYBRID must state which claims belong to which route. The plan is defeasible, not a pledge to produce a particular sign.

## Stage 2 — Research Question–Literature–Mechanism Map
For each closest relevant paper, give the precise question, players/timing/objectives, mechanism, actual result and proof domain, unresolved difference, and possible minimal change. Use an auditable table:
Question | Closest source and exact location | Existing mechanism and primitive | Unanswered distinction | Candidate model element | Falsifier / source reliability.
Compare conflicting results and identify which assumption explains their discrepancy; evaluate whether the claimed answer already follows from a general model. Normally compare 2–3 economically distinct designs; one is allowed if justified. Do not invent designs to satisfy a quota. Literature remains revisable after the solved model reveals new nearby theorems (Stages 4, 6). Deliver RESEARCH_QUESTION_LITERATURE_MAP.md or an identifiable equivalent section.

## Stage 3 — pre-model alignment (eight questions)
Record RQ-MECHANISM-ALIGNMENT in the existing Stage-3 output or a named companion:
1. What precisely is the one-sentence economic question, with outcomes that could refute the intuition?
2. What decisive economic feature, not just a topical label, does the question depend on?
3. Which player/choice/payoff/constraint/information/timing/externality/equilibrium term implements it?
4. Why is that term justified by a named source, institution or articulated economic logic?
5. Could a simpler application-neutral model already produce the same claimed result?
6. What minimal deletion, freezing, neutralization or substitution test would isolate the feature?
7. Which distinct possible results would count as answers (not only the favorable sign)?
8. What general economic principle, if any, could the findings illuminate?
Tag each response as PREDICTION, EVIDENCED or UNRESOLVED. Do not require a proof at Stage 3. If 2–6 are incoherent, route Stage 0/2/3 redesign before adding equations. Generality may itself be the object on MODEL-FIRST; thematic ablation may be NOT APPLICABLE with explicit reason.

## Stage 4 — comparable M0 / M1 / M2
Write the comparability contract before solving: players, move order, decision variables, objective/payoffs, constraint and externality, equilibrium concept, shock and outcome. M0 is the closest valid parent model; M1 is the minimum proposed change; M2 is a pre-specified diagnostic deletion/hold-fixed/substitution. Different choice sets or absent policy shock require matched/common-domain objects or a clearly non-comparable classification — never treat their difference as causal evidence. Track what is held constant, which outcome is measured and a falsifier. Separate a mechanism-identification ablation from a robustness perturbation. A thematic primitive may be ESSENTIAL to *transmission* but INTERPRETIVE to *the new sign reversal*; test the actual theorem claim, not an easier surrogate. If multiple M2s are needed, give nonredundant rationales and a budget. New structural complexity must buy explanatory power. Preserve exact algebra, numeric seeds/parameter domains, and non-results.

## Stage 6 — three independent contribution tests
A. THEOREM ABSORPTION: test renaming, change of variables, special case, corollary and minimal extension against the closest accessible prior statement, with source locations; status ABSORBED / APPARENTLY DISTINCT / UNRESOLVED.
B. MECHANISM CONTRIBUTION IDENTIFICATION: for each exact claim and domain, classify a proposed feature ESSENTIAL (needed for that claim under specified comparison), CONTRIBUTORY (materially changes domain/sign/welfare/predictions), INTERPRETIVE (primarily gives application meaning), UNRESOLVED. Distinguish a point witness from universal necessity. Report M0/M1/M2, source/proof/computation and strongest objection.
C. ECONOMIC LEARNING GAIN: independently say what economists newly learn relative to the parent: new mechanism, new result in known model, generalization, equilibrium existence/uniqueness/nonexistence, welfare/policy rule, substantive correction or important application. Score substantiation, not number of primitives. A and C can pass when B is INTERPRETIVE if claims honestly describe their general-theory/application contribution. Distinctness alone is not a publication case.

## Stage 7.5 and 7.5A — significance versus scope
Stage 7.5 independently evaluates question coverage, economic significance and claim-specific identification; produce CONTRIBUTION_IDENTIFICATION_REPORT.md (or equivalent identifiable section) with twelve required fields in templates/CONTRIBUTION_IDENTIFICATION_REPORT.md. An ESSENTIAL unanswered advertised question prevents unqualified GO even when math and novelty pass. Stage 7.5A separately tests generality/quantifiers and portability over meaningful model classes. Cournot-to-Bertrand survival is robustness evidence, not identification of an environmental externality. A theorem can be model-specific but important; keep a truthful model-specific claim. Stage 8 freeze records this report and its evidence version; changed result/question reopens implicated certification.

## Stage 11 — independent mismatch attacks
Independent evaluator reads the actual manuscript and proof/source anchors; attack (1) Generic Model in Disguise, (2) Research Question–Model Mismatch, (3) Nonessential Thematic Primitive, (4) Insufficient Economic Learning, (5) Alternative Model Challenge. For each: claim, best cited predecessor, exact model equation or theorem, meaningful counterfactual, severity FATAL / MAJOR BUT FIXABLE / MINOR / UNRESOLVED, earliest rollback Stage, and response evidence. An unresolved decisive identification question may never be closed by rewriting the Discussion. Preserve separate communication-only and mathematical referee gates.

## Stages 10A/10B/13 — explain supported mechanisms, not imagined mechanisms
Full-text exemplars are benchmarks of *argument architecture* (puzzle -> reason to model -> primitives -> countervailing channels -> theorem -> contribution -> limits), not mere polish. The manuscript's Introduction must match its actual theorem and literature; its Model must justify each decisive assumption; Results must explain channels with verified evidence; benchmarks must identify causal versus auxiliary primitives; Discussion and Conclusion must not extrapolate past the proof domain. If a narrative innovation requires a new theorem, reopen the research Stage; do not assert it in prose.

## Stages 12–15 — scientific risk versus venue fit
Maintain **five orthogonal scientific dimensions**: Mathematical Validity; Prior-Art Distinctness; Research Question Alignment; Economic Significance; Generality & Scope. Record PASS / LIMITED / UNRESOLVED / FAIL per axis with evidence, domain, and outstanding actions; the scientific axis states do not replace canonical Stage verdicts. Record journal/editorial fit separately. A limited but valid narrow theory can target a narrower outlet, without forced theory expansion.
Before new submissions, write PRE_SUBMISSION_SCIENTIFIC_RISK_BRIEF.md (or identifiable equivalent) with the strongest finding, parent comparison, thematic-feature role, strongest desk-reject attack, evidence-backed defense, unrun tests, unresolved scientific blockers, target justification and affirmative author review of exact snapshot. An unresolved **material core claim** is routed back to the earliest Stage; a journal threshold risk can be accepted with disclosure. Record author decision; never describe inferred acknowledgment as consent.

## Legacy and migration
v3.1 evidence and completed submissions remain historically immutable. This version never retroactively changes a GO or creates a withdrawal instruction. A legacy case used as a shadow regression is labelled POST-HOC DIAGNOSTIC and cannot be called a historical Stage failure unless the old rules and evidence then required the exact test. A later claim-specific scientific re-evaluation is a new assessment with its own date/record, not a rewrite.
