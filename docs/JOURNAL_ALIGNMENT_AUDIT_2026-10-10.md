# Independent audit: do early theoretical-novelty gates resemble actual economics journal editorial standards?
Date: 2026-10-10 JST; status **AUDIT FINDINGS ONLY**, no gate relaxation/criteria changes authorized by this report. Relevant draft PR #25, docs/NOVELTY_GATE_REGRESSION_CASES_2026-10-10.md (R1–R10), NOVELTY_ASSESSMENT_PHASED_EXTENSION_STANDARD.md.

## Verdict
**Broadly ALIGNED as a research ENTRY / limited-test SCREEN, not yet fully aligned as an early PUBLISHABILITY triage rule.** Correct adjustment: full journal-standard certification belongs AFTER proofs. But must cheaply check probable editorial economics value/audience/format BEFORE allocating significant modelling effort. The 10 regression cases largely test originality and mathematical absorption, not enough for journal-worthiness. Absence of proved absorption does not itself confer a strong full-paper prospect.

Important limitation: journal publisher descriptions/referee guidelines specify editorial preferences/standards, not a deterministic algorithm or actual acceptance probability. Evidence: editorial guidance plus original *accepted* theory examples, NOT private editorial decision letters, referee reports or quantified acceptances. Do not claim a formal matching of journal policies to an automated gate.

## Crucial real published theoretical extension examples
A. **Wang, Zeng & Zhu**, "The Core-Periphery Model Under Additively Separable Preferences", *Journal of Regional Science* 65(2), pp.378-402, 2025; published original open full text https://doi.org/10.1111/jors.12744. Changes CES to additively separable non-CES preference and discovers **demand adjustment effect**, procompetitive dispersion plus previously unanticipated location equilibria, multiple redispersions, bifurcations. This is a new-economic-result G/W example with a familiar core-periphery model; not just "one more utility function." Original full article Eq(1)-(4), intro and main equilibrium discussed and inspected.
B. **Edlin & Shannon**, "Strict Monotonicity in Comparative Statics", *Journal of Economic Theory* 81(1):201–219, 1998, https://doi.org/10.1006/jeth.1998.2405. Directly extends Milgrom–Shannon's known theory with sufficient conditions for strict monotonicity and policy/economic examples; publisher abstract inspected. Original theory extension has journal precedent.
C. **Baqaee & Burstein**, "Welfare and Output with Income Effects and Taste Shocks", *Quarterly Journal of Economics* 138(2):769–834, 2023, https://doi.org/10.1093/qje/qjac042. Extends Hulten's welfare-related theorem to nonhomothetic tastes/preference shocks and produces **new welfare vs standard income/output metric gap**, with economic applications; original full article read. G/W/U can be substantive without wholly new mechanism.
No extrapolation from 3 examples to guaranteed acceptance; publication cases demonstrate that specific kinds of extensions are acceptable.

## Four gaps in current early screening
**GAP1 Economic surprise/importance is underspecified.** "Economically important" in current Delta Card can be satisfied by rhetoric. Demand predicted DIFFERENCE IN UNDERSTANDING expressed without mathematics: what canonical conclusion, rank, advice, equilibrium or possibility changes and for whom? Distinguish generality for technical elegance versus real change in incentives/welfare. This is a prospective conjecture, not a proven fact.
**GAP2 Journal AUDIENCE & spatial relevance are late in existing pipeline.** For municipal industrial policy ask: why would a JRS/RSUE public/urban economist cite this? Does municipal choice cause the economic phenomenon, or can labels be removed? Do not require choosing a specific journal early; use plausible audience/family + one editorial sentence.
**GAP3 FULL ARTICLE vs NOTE/COMMENT publication units.** Economics Letters (thought-provoking example), JPET (short notes), JUE (Brief Notes) explicitly admit meaningful bounded contributions; "no full general theorem" is NOT automatic no-publication. Conversely note journals reject trivial pedagogy, and correction must be substantive. Stage0 can propose provisional FULL PAPER / NOTE / COMMENT / RESEARCH-ONLY, revisable at Stage6/12.
**GAP4 Theoretically testable ≠ probable full paper.** An unknown answer may be cheap to test but remote as journal headline; require two distinct early flags: "mathematical test worthwhile" and "editorially plausible IF successful" (strong/modest/highly speculative with reasons, no pseudo-probability scores). Route modest projects to cheap 1–2 theorem probes, not automatic full model. Explicit rival result may be close but not mathematically absorbed; editorial significance may still be weak. This is where hostile-editor VALUE checks belong before full investment, *without* forcing proof of novelty prematurely.

## Recommended 5-question early editorial-promise mini-screen (do NOT turn into extra hard Stage or require empirical data)
1. **What precise source-grounded theorem condition or previous predicted implication is being changed?**
2. **If the conjecture is TRUE, what important economic understanding changes in one natural-language sentence (market allocation, efficiency, comparative-static sign, governmental policy ranking, institutional implementability, or general applicability)?**
3. **What makes this effect relevant to the likely field readers (JRS geographic effect, RSUE spatial economic mechanism, JPET public welfare/institution, JET general theory)?** Do not force an exact journal selection this early.
4. **What is the strongest near paper arguing the result is obvious, fragile, uninteresting or already implicit?** Include both novelty and economic-importance threats.
5. **Can this be a meaningful full paper, note/comment, or only an internal theoretical observation if successful?** Choose provisional format and smallest credible mathematical probe; keep result UNPROVED.

Routing:
- **GO TO BOUNDED TEST** if a materially different, falsifiable parent-theorem delta, rational field interest, and affordable probe exist. Novelty and publication remain UNCERTIFIED.
- **CONDITIONAL GO ON SPECIFIC EVIDENCE** if a decisive source fact prevents credible judgement, one stated blocker only per current workflow.
- **NO-GO to substantial model** if zero economic understanding changes even if a formula changes, or demonstrably duplicated result. A cheap counterexample or note hypothesis may still warrant preliminary investigation when it has a sharp surprising implication.
- **Research-only** legitimate endpoint if mathematical/educational benefit exists but no supported economic journal value; not a pejorative category.
No numerical publication-likelihood score. A journal's acceptance rate or impact factor would not identify viability of one paper.

## Important conclusion to communicate
The 10 original regression cases are good *false-novelty/false-NO-GO control tests* but **are NOT a sufficient journal publication heuristic**. Existing later Stage 6/7.5/11/12 editorial completeness checks need an EARLY, low-cost preview, not transfer of publication-proof obligation to Stage0. The revised workflow is sensible as a research start standard, yet can still authorize too many unimportant toy generalizations unless this extra significance+audience+format triage is included. Full-math/referee strictness remains unchanged.
