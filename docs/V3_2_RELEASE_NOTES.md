# research-paper-workflow v3.2 — Research Question–Mechanism Alignment & Economic Model Design

Release date: 2026-11-19. Author-approved MINOR release on the unchanged v3.0 stage architecture; successor to v3.1.

## Motivation and scientific distinction
A mathematically valid or unabsorbed theorem is not by itself evidence that an advertised theme-specific primitive causes the result. Conversely, a valuable general theorem, correction, new result in a known model, or important application must not be rejected solely because it lacks theme-specific causal structure. v3.2 explicitly separates prior-art theorem distinctness, research-question alignment, claim-specific mechanism role, incremental economic learning, and provable scope.

## Changes
- Stage 0: THEME-FIRST / MODEL-FIRST / HYBRID research starts and evidence-backed pivots.
- Stages 2–3: question–literature–mechanism mapping; eight pre-model alignment questions.
- Stages 4–6: comparable pre-specified parent/minimal/counterfactual models (M0/M1/M2), theorem absorption, per-claim and domain ESSENTIAL / CONTRIBUTORY / INTERPRETIVE / UNRESOLVED thematic roles, and economic-learning assessment.
- Stages 7.5/7.5A/8: contribution-identification handoff, with mechanism identification separated from robustness, generality and freeze scope.
- Stage 11: five independent model–question mismatch/referee attacks and evidence-backed rollback.
- Stages 10A/10B/12–15: manuscript argument tracing, five separate scientific assessments, distinct journal fit and an author-reviewed submission scientific-risk brief.
- Updated governing documents, existing templates/checklists, a lightweight contribution-report template and a pre-submission scientific risk brief. Equivalent clearly named Stage-report sections are permitted to prevent paperwork multiplication.
- Regression cases A–F, including a non-destructive post-hoc Green Subsidies audit with five pinned original document blob hashes. Primary-source research-methodology and independent review reports accompany the implementation.
