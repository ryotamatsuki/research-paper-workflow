# Novelty screening decision regression cases — 2026-10-10

Applies to Pre-Stage F; Stages 0, 2, 3, 4, 6 and referee stages. Draft verification protocol; examples are hypothetical/known-paper-adjacent diagnostic cases, NOT certified theorem outcomes.

| Case | Ex ante evidence | Expected screening decision | Post-proof effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| R1 Existing source directly proves exact candidate theorem | Original proposition and conditions read; result follows by explicit variable map | NO-GO for that SAME CLAIM at Stage2 | Not revived by new application nouns; can only re-enter with a truly different Delta Card |
| R2 New theorem might hold under weaker economically important assumptions | Parent proposition/assumptions verified, no adverse immediate general theorem mapped, feasible finite proof probe | Stage0/2 GO to bounded Stage3/4 test (conjecture explicitly UNPROVED) | Stage6 strict compare actual proven scope with all general theorems |
| R3 Meaningful sharp boundary is not yet established | Parent result known, one proposed failure/existence cutoff matters economically, counterexample probe specified | GO to bounded attempt despite no new strategic actor | Certify proof and check threshold already known; kill if tautology or same boundary |
| R4 New welfare/ranking or equilibrium result within well-known model class | Material economic question, parent and two closest rivals considered; precise predicted result unproved | GO for bounded theory even if all primitives are familiar | Stage6 reject if existing general theorem immediately implies actual result |
| R5 "The model is just contract theory/public goods" but precise result not mapped | Closest paper uses same class, but no theorem specialization demonstrating identical conclusion | Literature status CLOSE/HIGH RISK, NOT automatic absorbed; acquire source or bounded screening consistent with access | Stage6 fail-closed if mapping unresolved; never say "certified new" on search failure |
| R6 Decisive 2026 paper only original abstract accessible | Exactly one parent theorem materially determines novelty, no readable equations | CONDITIONAL GO only to specified source acquisition, no Stage4 bypass | Original full theorem may kill result after inspection |
| R7 Model adds another industry/state, but no different welfare/equilibrium result or real assumption weakening | No falsifiable substantive Delta Card and only copy of old model | NO-GO for economic emptiness without need to prove exact absorption | No manuscript |
| R8 Tiny test yields familiar local-public-good free-riding and no distinct proposition | Complete diagnostic local best responses and planner solution show standard generic underprovision | NO-GO for THAT specific proposition, preserve counterexample/regression | Do not claim all future models with the same parent are impossible |
| R9 Earlier contribution used to be rejected for "no new mechanism", but has a substantive original theorem candidate | Delta Card specifies new sharp theorem boundary, named source differences, finite test | Permit reconsideration only for a NEW precisely DIFFERENT result, with provenance/rollback; prior negative result preserved | Math/novelty certificates not inherited from rejected branch |
| R10 Formula derivable as 2-line corollary of already published stronger theorem | Original math and mapping verified at early or late stage | Early NO-GO if known before math effort; otherwise strict Stage6 theorem NO-GO | May still have application/policy interpretation value, but no unsupported theory originality claim |

## Applied-economic-theory regression extensions (research-track dependent)

| Case | Ex ante situation | Correct APPLIED THEORY treatment |
|---|---|---|
| AR1 | New abstract mathematical theorem, identical economic predictions and no economically material interpretation | **NO-GO in applied track**, even if general theorem originality is plausible; a pure-theory track can evaluate separately |
| AR2 | Known principal-agent class but source-grounded conjecture changes meaningful taxpayer welfare, regional industry policy or firm behavior | **GO to bounded test**, conditional on no demonstrated known-result absorption; economic importance and reader community established |
| AR3 | Important policy description without an unresolved endogenous choice/incentive/welfare tradeoff | **NO-GO for theory research**, though it may be useful policy reporting |
| AR4 | Significant new theoretical explanation of municipal policy failure, no implementable corrective instrument provided | **Potential GO** as positive/diagnostic applied theory; require meaningful mechanism and eventual source/novelty proof |
| AR5 | Desired policy reversal artificially imposed by unexplained income/rent-share coefficient | **NO-GO for economic microfoundation**, even if equations formally solve |
| AR6 | Weaker-assumption existence theorem brings empirically plausible institutions into domain and changes predictable equilibrium/welfare cases | **GO to bounded extension test** on economic value; Stage6 re-kill after proof |

**Regression invariant:** The ten R1–R10 examples test source/novelty decision; these AR examples ensure that the applied project never mistakes *mathematical curiosity as a separate source of applied-paper value*. Model tractability/correctness and publication value are not two coequal interests: they are method requirements and economic-contribution review for a single applied research question.

## Acceptance invariants
1. Do not assert a paper contribution merely because Stage0–3 GO.
2. Do not require proof of a novel theorem as an INVESTMENT gate.
3. Reject material mathematical mistakes, incorrect global equilibrium, arbitrary rent shares and prior solved results whenever demonstrated.
4. Detect original-journal / WP model differences and access limitations instead of fabricating proof of absence.
5. Distinguish *component prior art*, *whole-game prior art*, *specific theorem/result absorption* and *economic significance*.
6. No exact-new-mechanism condition applied uniformly to all G/B/W/U/C contributions.
7. Stage6 and Stage11 keep original adversarial novel-theorem requirements; Stage4A/7.5A proofs/freeze unchanged.
8. Preserve earlier failed cases without "retrospective GO" unless a separately justified new economic proposition is being tested.
