# Mandatory pre-writing exemplary-paper benchmark — design record (2026-11-17)

Status: proposed v3.0 MAJOR-class candidate, not released. Stable v2.9 is unchanged. User requested a new stage after observing that an already drafted theory manuscript would have been better structured if strong published exemplars had been studied before writing.

The repository already contained an unreleased editorial exemplar requirement inside old Stage 10. It allowed research to begin before benchmarking and emphasized specific rhetorical moves rather than full-paper construction/proof style. This change promotes the prerequisite into Stage 10A, extends selection to an explicitly comparative evidence-bearing rubric, and calls old manuscript construction Stage 10B while retaining its template filename. Existing scientific gates and canonical verdict meanings stay intact. Author-only approvals, freeze and formal certificate controls are unchanged. Normal route: Stage 9 → Stage 10A → Stage 10B → Stage 11. Stage 10A CONDITIONAL GO does not permit drafting; material scientific discoveries route to their original gate.

Migration: historical Stages 10/11+ reports remain historical; current in-flight projects with frozen science and existing draft may enter new Stage 10A before rewriting/revalidating 10B, without falsely backdating exemplars or resetting stages 0–9. A published old paper is not retrospectively invalidated. Existing references to Stage 10 design/build/referee repair in historical release records and older templates mean Stage 10B unless explicitly addressing the benchmark; use new names for current decisions. No new stable release, tag or release automation is authorized by this integration PR.

Regression cases (expected outcomes; not empirical proof of improved manuscripts):
1. A famous unrelated paper with many citations but no observed useful construction loses to a directly applicable, well-written field paper; justify choice from inspected passages.
2. A DOI/abstract, PDF URL, preview or AI summary alone is not acquisition. Exclude unavailable preferred candidate before scoring and immediately substitute an eligible one; if an eligible set has complete essential role coverage, no access-based hold. If not after bounded substitution, Stage 10A remains blocked.
3. A manuscript compiles but Stage 10A is missing → 10B not authorized, no Stage 11 shortcut.
4. Elegant-looking shortened proof drops a globality assumption → scientific rollback rather than presentation PASS.
5. Three credible candidates genuinely cover all required roles but a fourth adds no value → acceptable; fewer require justification and minimum one full-text primary.
6. Actual draft departs from primary model to preserve the project's key welfare benchmark → justified adaptation, not automatic failure.
7. Stage 11 finds key intuition buried, despite mathematically valid results → bounded repair 10B/13 and independent retest; missing exemplar selection evidence → 10A.
8. Target changes at Stage 12 → refresh comparator and editorial instructions, no automatic theory expansion.
9. Previously drafted project adopts new gate → conduct 10A prospectively, rewrite/retest 10B; retain history and freeze unless substantive claims change.
10. A new major formal/novelty issue is discovered while reading exemplars → earliest affected scientific gate; 10A is neither novelty nor formal certificate.

Validation planned: verify linked files and canonical hard route/entry/exit/rollback across README, governance, pipeline and Stage-9–14 templates; static checks only until full integration independently reviewed. No new release publication. Existing post-v2.9 refinements and historical release records retained.

## Full-text acquisition filter — 2026-11-17 follow-up

**Full-text acquisition is a hard eligibility filter before scoring or selecting any primary or complementary writing exemplar.** Obtain the complete article and any appendices/supplements material to assessing its proofs or writing roles through lawful accessible sources (publisher, author/institutional repository, or legitimately supplied copy). An identifiable author accepted manuscript is acceptable when sufficiently complete and faithful to the published article; record its version and discrepancies. A PDF or complete HTML/text accessed and read through a supported tool is equally acceptable; local PDF download is not mandatory. Verify actual content access and completeness (first/last page or section, section/appendix inventory, no material gaps), then read the complete text including material proof sections and exhibits. A link, abstract, search snippets, AI summary, partial preview or an untested PDF URL is not acquisition. Log source, access date, version, coverage and locators; retain a lawful copy only where permitted, never republish copyrighted PDFs by default. If acquisition fails, mark the candidate EXCLUDED — FULL TEXT UNAVAILABLE, record why, and immediately advance to the next suitable candidate within a bounded search; do not score it or retain it as a selected exemplar, and do not hold the gate open merely to obtain a preferred famous paper. Require a fully acquired/read primary plus sufficiently complete verified complementary role coverage; fewer than 3 papers permitted with a documented bounded-search justification. Only if no eligible set covers essential roles after reasonable substitution may a material blocker prevent GO. This filter applies to writing exemplars only; decisive inaccessible prior art remains subject to the separate fail-closed novelty/source-audit rules.

Regression additions: (a) a highly cited inaccessible candidate and a complete accessible field paper: only the latter eligible; (b) all main pages accessible through a supported HTML/PDF reader plus material appendices, but local download fails: eligible after complete reading; (c) accessible main article with a missing material proof supplement: exclude and substitute; (d) only two complete papers cover every essential writing role: justified smaller set may GO; (e) a decisive closest prior theorem is inaccessible: separate novelty gate unchanged, do not treat access-based exemplar exclusion as novelty evidence. Existing in-flight reports remain historical; rerun acquisition/selection under this rule without resetting frozen scientific work. These are expected regression scenarios, not empirical validation.
