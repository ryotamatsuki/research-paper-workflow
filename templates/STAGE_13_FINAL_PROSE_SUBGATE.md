# Stage 13 — Independent Complete-PDF Publication Prose Subgate (introduced v3.1, retained v3.2)

**Inside existing Stage 13 after all integration edits; NOT Stage 13P.** Stage 11's prior GO certifies a draft, not the later completed manuscript.

## Snapshot and independence

Freeze actual submission-facing complete manuscript PDF SHA-256, source commit SHA, page count, appendix/supplement and declaration coverage. Record reviewer identity/type, access sequence, prior exposure and actual pages inspected. Blind first reading means **only the complete PDF and neutral target journal/article type**, without Stage-11/13 notes, author intent, exemplar map or solutions. Disclose unavoidable prior model exposure. AI critical review is not human peer review.

## Phase A: independent cold all-page read

Read Abstract, Introduction, model, results and neighboring intuition, robustness/welfare, related literature, Discussion, Conclusion, captions/figures/tables, proofs and appendices, bibliography and declarations. Before prior explanations, independently paraphrase in 120–200 words the economic puzzle, result, which variable moves and what stays fixed, opposing economic forces, the precise theorem boundary and substantive novelty.

Write a **strongest credible communication-only desk-reject paragraph**, separate from mathematical, priority and journal-fit objections. For each problem provide an exact short quote, PDF page/section/paragraph, MAJOR/MINOR/FATAL EDITORIAL severity, why a qualified reader is impeded, bounded repair and scientific guardrail. Actively test unnatural journal English, paragraph transitions, duplicated defensive novelty caveats, internal workflow/CI history, proof bridge presentation and statements/declarations. Do **not** ban genuine economic-game Stage 1, necessary replication facts or theorem limitations.

**Freeze and save FINAL_PROSE_REFEREE_REPORT.md before showing sources, prior editorial notes or an issue answer key.**

## Phase B: inspected published-paper comparison

After Phase A has been frozen, reveal only genuine Stage-10A exemplars fully acquired/read including material appendices (and Stage-12 journal-specific refresh). Abstracts, snippets and inaccessible URLs are **not** eligible. Compare exact source version/section/page/paragraph against exact current draft location for: opening problem/why-care, transitions and prose economy, scholarly English, model/proof exposition, theorem-adjacent opposing mechanism intuition, original contribution and fair prior-work credit, exhibits, Discussion/limits and whole-paper unity. Mark STRONG / ADEQUATE / WEAK / NOT APPLICABLE with actual observed evidence, not prestige. Never imitate protected source prose or import its theorem/empirical claims. Save FINAL_PROSE_EXEMPLAR_COMPARISON.md.

## Repair and independent second full reading

Maintain FINAL_PROSE_ISSUE_REGISTER.md: finding ID, exact location and quotation, severity, bounded repair, before/after diff, owner, scientific scope safeguard, status and closure test. Keep the theorem assumptions, negative evidence, counterexamples, quantitative results and accurate AI/author verification declarations. Discovery of a substantive problem returns to the earliest scientific Stage.

After all material corrections, rebuild PDF, record new source SHA/PDF SHA-256, and commission a **second independent critical reader** to cold-read the **entire revised paper** without the solution key. An original editor or same drafting agent may check minor mechanical diffs but cannot alone close disputed MAJOR items. Record independence, coverage and issue closures in FINAL_PROSE_REVISION_AUDIT.md. Reports can be combined only with separately identifiable phases and immutable timestamps.

## Existing Stage-13 verdict, no new Stage

**GO:** ordered, evidence-backed all-PDF first read and fully inspected exemplars, all material defects repaired, independent second complete reading, no unexplained WEAK role, accepted exact source SHA/PDF hash. **CONDITIONAL GO:** exactly one bounded editorial fix/retest pending; cannot progress to Stage 14. **NO-GO:** several material flaws, incomplete reading, unavailable exemplar coverage, compromised ordering, false blindness, failed second reading or hash mismatch. Route back to Stage 13/10A or to the earliest scientific Stage for science drift.

Stage 14 verifies **that exact accepted snapshot**; every post-GO text edit including captions, appendix and AI/Data/Code declarations needs targeted or full independent re-review. CI confirms document structure only, never actual prose quality or acceptance likelihood.
