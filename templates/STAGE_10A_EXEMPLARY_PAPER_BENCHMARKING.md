# Stage 10A — Exemplary Paper Benchmarking & Synthesis

> Mandatory pre-writing gate. Canonical authority: GOVERNANCE.md → THEORY_PAPER_RESEARCH_PIPELINE.md → this template. v3.0 mandatory canonical route after Stage 9 and before Stage 10B.

## Role
Act as a specialist economics researcher, mathematical expositor and critical journal editor. Identify published writing models outstanding in the relevant research tradition and analyze their actual construction. Do not claim unanimous economist preference.

## Inputs
- Stage 8 freeze ID, claim/proof register, portability/scope/benchmark certificates.
- Stage 9 GO, repository and reproducibility contract.
- Article type, certified contribution, relevant field and methods, closest-literature register; provisional journal (if known).
- Apply `checklists/EXEMPLARY_PAPER_BENCHMARKING_CHECKLIST.md`.

## Execution
1. Define search criteria and finite budget before searching (start with 8–15 plausible candidates; adapt when field is narrow). Search authoritative publisher and author versions, references/forward links, leading field and general venues; record search paths, dates and selection/exclusion.
2. Apply the full-text acquisition filter **before** scoring or shortlisting.

**Full-text acquisition is a hard eligibility filter before scoring or selecting any writing exemplar.** Obtain the complete article and any appendices/supplements material to assessing its proofs or writing roles through lawful accessible sources (publisher, author/institutional repository, or legitimately supplied copy). An identifiable author accepted manuscript is acceptable when sufficiently complete and faithful to the published article; record its version and discrepancies. A PDF or complete HTML/text accessed and read through a supported tool is equally acceptable; local PDF download is not mandatory. Verify actual content access and completeness (first/last page or section, section/appendix inventory, no material gaps), then read the complete text including material proof sections and exhibits. A link, abstract, search snippets, AI summary, partial preview or an untested PDF URL is not acquisition. Log source, access date, version, coverage and locators; retain a lawful copy only where permitted, never republish copyrighted PDFs by default. If acquisition fails, mark the candidate EXCLUDED — FULL TEXT UNAVAILABLE, record why, and immediately advance to the next suitable candidate within a bounded search; do not score it or retain it as a selected exemplar, and do not hold the gate open merely to obtain a preferred famous paper. Require a fully acquired/read exemplar set with complete verified coverage of essential writing roles; no overall primary paper is mandatory; fewer than 3 papers permitted with a documented bounded-search justification. Only if no eligible set covers essential roles after reasonable substitution may a material blocker prevent GO. This filter applies to writing exemplars only; decisive inaccessible prior art remains subject to the separate fail-closed novelty/source-audit rules.

Record eligibility as FULL TEXT ACQUIRED / EXCLUDED — FULL TEXT UNAVAILABLE; reading as COMPLETE / INCOMPLETE. Only acquired and completely read candidates enter the scored selection.
3. Apply the seven-dimensional rubric. Justify component scores against observed evidence; record uncertainty/disagreement. Normally select 3–5 distinct papers; assign roles based on source-located strengths. A smaller set (including a single paper) is allowed only after justified bounded search, comparison, complete acquisition/reading and complete material coverage. An overall anchor is optional. Rank is not merit or consensus.
4. Extract specific transferable moves without reproducing prose. Explain why a move fits the frozen paper, what cannot be transferred and how the claim's assumptions, proof-critical bridges, negative results and scope remain visible.
5. Create the blueprint below **before drafting**. Test it against the exact freeze and use a critical specialist read-through of its plan. Do not pretend this is review of an unwritten manuscript. No claim changes or new proof without appropriate scientific rollback.

## Role-wise benchmarking, synthesis and coherence audit (mandatory)

The unit of selection is the writing technique, not the whole paper. Normally use complementary strengths from several eligible full-text exemplars; an overall anchor paper is optional. Total rubric scores inform screening only: do not mechanically choose the highest total or highest subscore, or assume a top-journal style fits every article type. For each applicable role (overall structure, Introduction and problem framing, clarity and prose economy, notation/model and theorem architecture, proof strategy and bridge visibility, economic intuition and post-result interpretation, novelty/contribution framing, figures/tables/examples, Discussion/conclusion and limitations), compare specific source-located techniques and choose the best fit for the frozen contribution. Multiple sources may support a single role; one source may support several roles. Document alternatives rejected, comparative reasons, limitations, and why a role is inapplicable if relevant. The preferred outcome is an original synthesis that can improve on individual exemplars where justified, not a claim of demonstrated superiority or imitation of source prose.

Include a role-selection matrix in EDITORIAL_EXEMPLAR_MAP.md (role; eligible candidates and exact locations; observed strengths/tradeoffs; selected move(s); project-specific adaptation; rejected alternatives and reasons; scientific guardrails). Integrate all adopted moves into MANUSCRIPT_BLUEPRINT.md. Before GO, explicitly audit whole-paper coherence: one economic narrative and contribution hierarchy; consistent notation, definitions, terminology and voice; compatible theorem/lemma and proof sequencing; no duplicated sections or intuition; figures serve the argument; main-text/appendix allocation preserves proof-critical bridges; transitions and limits preserve exact frozen claims, assumptions and negative results. Resolve conflicts between techniques by choosing an internally coherent article-specific architecture, not by copying or mechanically splicing sections. Document the conflict, alternatives, decision and justification; if no conflict, record tests and result. A single-paper solution requires evidence that alternative techniques were considered and it covers every material role without sacrificing fit. No unresolved material coherence or coverage defect may pass Stage 10A.

Stage 10B records exact implementation and justified deviations for each selected role. Stage 11 independently attacks both role-specific quality and the integrated paper's coherence after a cold read, not merely similarity to exemplars. Stage 12 recalibrates roles for the journal using the full-text filter; Stage 13 repairs and retests role quality and coherence; Stage 14 verifies the exact final PDF retains the resolved synthesis. Scientific defects return to the earliest scientific gate; never simplify a proof by silently changing its scope.

### MANUSCRIPT_BLUEPRINT.md
- Exact freeze ID, article type, provisional venue, main economic question and distinctive finding.
- Ordered section roles, reader-arrival targets, opening narrative (puzzle → stakes → mechanism → result → contribution), literature and contribution placement.
- For each headline result: statement priority, definitions, economic intuition, necessary intermediate objects/bridge equations, lemma/proof path, body versus appendix, reproducible exhibit/example, certified conditions and negative evidence.
- Proof design audit: where an invariant, construction, decomposition, direct argument, necessary/sufficient split or concise lemma may shorten exposition without changing certified content. If not valid, retain original proof. No artificial simplification.
- Figure/table plan: primary visual vehicle, data/analytic source, axis and scope; five-exhibit thought experiment where applicable.
- Original prose/notation policy, discussion/conclusion plan, cold-reader contract and Stage 12 refresh plan.
- Blueprint decisions traced to exact exemplar evidence; justified deviations as needed. No manuscript prose draft necessary at this gate.

## Gate
- GO — full-text acquisition and complete reading verified for every selected exemplar, with material role coverage, comparative role selection and coherence audit verified, comparative assessment and implementable blueprint saved, no material blockers; authorize Stage 10B only.
- CONDITIONAL GO — exactly one material blocker and bounded 10A repair; no 10B authorization.
- NO-GO — stop this route pending justified re-entry; no drafting.
Independent Stage 11 must attack actual writing with cold reader and benchmark evidence; Stage 12 refresh, Stage 13 retest, Stage 14 exact PDF verification. Log AI provenance and source-access limitations. This is not peer review, a novelty certificate or a submission recommendation.
