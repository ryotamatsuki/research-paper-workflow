# Phased novelty assessment and legitimate extensions of prior theory
**2026-10-10 draft workflow refinement.** Governing authority: GOVERNANCE.md, THEORY_PAPER_RESEARCH_PIPELINE.md, this protocol. It changes early research-investment screening, NOT rigorous late originality proof or mathematical certification.

## Clarification: mathematical economics generalization has legitimate disciplinary worth

The applied project can prioritize its own subject without deeming all otherwise significant GENERAL ECONOMIC THEORY results valueless. A weaker-assumption existence theorem or a uniform method for generalizing economic games can matter to mathematical economics even if it does not reverse a single municipal welfare comparison. Broad economic-theory contribution and local-project fit are separate descriptors. OUTSIDE PROJECT leads to DEFER/ROUTE, not scholarly NO-GO. See [docs/GENERALIZATION_SCHOLARLY_VALUE_AUDIT_2026-10-10.md](docs/GENERALIZATION_SCHOLARLY_VALUE_AUDIT_2026-10-10.md). Journal of Mathematical Economics requires actual economic-theory content, not immediate applied-policy prescriptions; TE publishes general theory. All post-proof originality and economics-journal significance requirements remain.

## 0. Applied-economic-theory priority rule (project track override)

Where the research project is **APPLIED ECONOMICS using economic theory**, the primary entry criterion is NOT "is the mathematics worth doing?" in isolation. The sequence is:
**economically important explanatory/welfare/policy question → predicted, UNPROVED new economic understanding → fit of mathematical modelling and finite test**.

A mathematically interesting theorem with no substantive change in economists' understanding does not count as an applied-economics GO. An original new result within an established mathematical class may be worth a bounded test if it changes a meaningful regional/industrial allocation, incentive, welfare or institutional conclusion. Mathematics is a method; tractability is a necessary execution constraint and correctness is later strictly audited. Do not demand econometrics, calibration or practical prescription as a universal condition for applied economic theory. A sharp explanation of policy failure may suffice as positive/diagnostic contribution.

For this track use [APPLIED_ECONOMIC_THEORY_TRACK.md](APPLIED_ECONOMIC_THEORY_TRACK.md) and the six-field Applied Theory Contribution Card. Cheap, preliminary field-audience and article-type checks are part of establishing **economic question significance**, not an unrelated equally-weighted "editorial worth" score. The prior two-axis suggestion in docs/JOURNAL_ALIGNMENT_AUDIT_2026-10-10.md is superseded for this track.

## 1. What the September 2026 novelty tuning already got right
GitHub commit 51bd722633a79020294991737625e94ba10ccde6 (September 2, 2026) introduced component vs whole-game absorption, and generalization/unification as a valid contribution route. Current v2.8 Stage 2 already says familiar individual components do not automatically absorb a full game, and current GOVERNANCE.md section 2.19 already recognizes substantive new results in known models, generalizations, corrections and unifications. **Do not undo these principles.**

The October failure is **operational**, not merely absence of a novelty category. At an ex ante stage we repeatedly required proof-like evidence that a new theorem already existed before allowing a bounded theoretical investigation. Stage 3 language privileged a new interaction arising only from full joint endogeneity, thereby penalizing valuable generalizations of existing results, sharp validity boundaries, counterexamples and new welfare/comparative-static conclusions in familiar mathematical model classes. An overly restrictive Pre-Stage F also implied that a theorem-level novelty gap must be proved before a conjectured extension can even enter Stage 0.

## 2. Six legitimate theoretical contribution routes
- **G: Generalization.** Weaken an economically binding assumption, enlarge the theorem domain or prove the same key economic conclusion under materially weaker conditions. A new strategic mechanism is NOT required. But if an existing more general theorem trivially entails it, it is absorbed.
- **B: Boundary / counterexample.** Locate a sharp validity condition, precise failure domain or instructive counterexample to a known theorem, with a meaningful economic consequence. An arbitrary income split or tautological threshold is not enough.
- **W: New equilibrium / welfare / comparative statics / policy result.** In an existing model class, derive an unproved equilibrium branch, robust comparative-static sign, policy ranking, implementability result, or resident-welfare implication. New mathematical notation, mechanical accounting or a numerical witness misrepresented as a universal theorem are not enough.
- **U: Substantive unification.** Recover several leading previous benchmarks within a theorem and establish a meaningful cross-model result that was not already a simple corollary.
- **M: Distinct economic mechanism.** Identify an original causal/strategic channel with a non-absorbed theoretical result; this is one route, not the universally required route.
- **C: Substantive correction/reassessment.** Establish and correct a consequential error or scope claim in a leading model after checking all prior corrections. Cosmetic algebra alone is not sufficient.
These categories are legitimate contribution routes, but **for an applied economic theory project an economically substantive payoff is REQUIRED BEFORE committing to a mathematical test**. Later publication completeness is still checked rigorously, yet economics significance cannot be postponed until after proofs.

## 3. Different evidence burdens at DIFFERENT STAGES
### Pre-Stage F: evidence-led source atlas
Map existing results, original theorem conditions, key assumptions, author-stated limitations, and closest adjacent theories. **Annotate source-grounded extension windows**, not claims that every omission is an unsolved theorem. A specific promising window can go to Stage 0 for limited testing without requiring a new theorem to be proved in Pre-Stage F. Access limitations and strongest near-miss papers are logged, never disguised as proof of absence. Zero candidates is acceptable, but do not set a proof-of-novelty entrance bar that precludes research itself.

### Stage 0: Delta Card, not novelty certificate
Require a named parent proposition and its assumptions, one economically motivated change, an explicitly UNPROVED hypothesized result of type G/B/W/U/M/C, strongest near-miss literature, intended welfare/economic significance and **smallest bounded falsification experiment**. GO at this stage means worth source/math audit, not that a new theorem exists. No obligation for the result to exist only in a larger joint-endogeneity game.

### Stage 1: exact original source
Verify parent original mathematical statement, assumptions, complete strategic choices and reported proof/version boundaries. Reconstruct nested benchmark and exact differences. If an existing original paper explicitly proves the proposed delta, kill that contribution; otherwise ambiguity is not yet negative novelty certification.

### Stage 2: ex ante originality and investment eligibility
**This stage does not certify a novel proven theorem.** For each Delta Card record one:
1. **DIRECTLY ABSORBED:** identify an actual theorem and a result-level mapping/specialization proving that the proposed economic conclusion is an immediate known corollary; or specific source directly proves the same conclusion. Canonical NO-GO for that contribution.
2. **PROMISING UNPROVED EXTENSION:** source-anchored economically material delta, a plausible different conclusion and a bounded mathematical probe, with no demonstrated absorption after closest-paper checks. Canonical GO to *limited* Stage 3/4 exploration, explicitly without a novelty guarantee.
3. **ONE MATERIAL SOURCE OR DERIVATION BLOCKER:** credible threat but decisive source/math not yet checked. Canonical CONDITIONAL GO only to the narrowly specified closing action, preserving existing exactly-one-blocker contract.
4. **NO MATERIAL ECONOMIC DELTA:** no falsifiable proposition or hypothesis, or obvious tautology/mechanical results from defensible primitives. Canonical NO-GO.
Similarity to a known *general theory class*, an abstract mentioning principal-agent, or separate known components **is a high-risk warning, not proof of an exact absorption claim**. Claim-specific actual theorem mapping is required to call a plausible extension directly absorbed. Inaccessibility does not prove novelty; a tiny mathematical probe can be used only after stage-appropriate source gate closure.

### Stage 3: limited theorem-probe candidate search
Candidates may seek a weaker assumption, sharper boundary, new result within a known model, substantive unification, or distinct mechanism. Use a bounded, explicit mathematical falsification plan and nested parent benchmark. A temporary symbolic check or narrow example is permitted as **diagnostic-only**, not a full valid paper theorem or permission to bypass Stage 4. Do not require simultaneous endogeneity or a novel full-game architecture for every project.

### Stages 4–5 / 4A: solve and independently attack
Full equilibrium/participation/financing/corner/global deviations and mathematical certificate requirements are UNCHANGED. A proven failure of one conjecture is retained as NO-GO for that specific result, not a license to declare all other extensions of a parent impossible.

### Stage 6: strict actual-result novelty certificate
Re-check the **actual derived propositions**, not ex ante speculation, against explicit original-theorem mapping. A result that is a short corollary of an existing general theorem fails the original-theory headline even if application wording differs. A G/B/W/U/C contribution may survive without an unprecedented mechanism or full-game-only interaction if it proves materially weaker hypotheses, a sharp previously unknown validity boundary, a new welfare or equilibrium conclusion, or a meaningful unification. Failure to reconstruct a parent does NOT imply originality. Stage 7.5, 7.5A and Stage 11 retain independent proof/quantifier/economic importance/editorial red-team standards.

## 4. Decision safeguards and bounded search
- NO-GO for "this is already a known result" requires a named parent theorem or demonstrably equivalent solved result and an explicit mapping. A strong abstract-only near-miss remains a reason for targeted source acquisition, not blanket fatal novelty absorption.
- NO-GO for "no worthwhile economic effect" requires actual argument that the candidate conjecture is tautological or lacks material economics; independent justification from direct theorem absorption is not necessary here.
- A small mathematically tractable extension probe is a legitimate exploration investment, not an automatic author permission to write a paper.
- Preserve earlier proven counterexamples and negative results; reopen ONLY a materially different Delta Card with a separately justified new possible economic conclusion. An earlier generic municipal supplier switching free-riding NO-GO and public procurement generic monitoring NO-GO do NOT magically reverse.
- Existing finite exploration budget, GO/CONDITIONAL GO/NO-GO and rollback rules are preserved.
- Sample Delta Card fields: parent DOI/version, theorem/proposition and assumptions, contribution type, new assumption/margin, UNPROVED prediction, strongest rival/theorem, precise alleged absorption map or UNKNOWN, minimum mathematical probe, economic significance, stop criterion, next action.

## 5. Concrete old-versus-new rule
**September v1.1** added VALID CONCEPTS: ingredient overlap vs full-game result, acceptable unification/generalization. **October revision** adds OPERATIONAL EXECUTION: no publication-strength novelty proof before testing; stronger evidence for *negative originality assertions*; explicit support for theorem generalizations/boundaries/new welfare results without new joint endogeneity; strict post-proof Stage 6. This prevents "new model must invent a new economic mechanism" from covertly overriding the v1.1 principle.

This does not lower the standard for a paper to be published. It corrects the point in the research sequence at which the strict standard is applied.
